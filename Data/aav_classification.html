2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2022 ACR/EULAR classification criteria for ANCA-associated vasculitis (GPA, MPA, EGPA) provide standardized diagnostic categorization for clinical research and specialized clinical care, ensuring accurate differentiation between necrotizing small-vessel vasculitides.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Small-vessel inflammation driven by antineutrophil cytoplasmic antibodies (PR3-ANCA and MPO-ANCA) activating neutrophils.
