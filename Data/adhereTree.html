ADHERE In-Hospital Mortality Risk Tree (Acute HF)

Calculation ID: adhereTree
1. Formula & Parameters
Classification and regression tree (CART) based on BUN, SBP, and Creatinine
Normal Range / Target: BUN < 43 mg/dL

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Key nodes: BUN ≥43 mg/dL, SBP <115 mmHg, Creatinine ≥2.75 mg/dL.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Rapid emergency department risk stratification for acute decompensated heart failure.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Decompensated Heart Failure

Key Medical Decision: Identifies high-risk patients requiring immediate ICU admission and aggressive diuresis/inotropes.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Hepatorenal and vasopressor collapse markers in acute pump failure.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Fonarow et al. (JAMA, 2005).
