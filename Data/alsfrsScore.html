ALSFRS-R (ALS Functional Rating Scale)

Calculation ID: alsfrsScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 12 items across 4 domains (Bulbar, Fine Motor, Gross Motor, Respiratory; 0–48)
Normal Range / Target: 48 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • 4 domains scored 0–12 each (Bulbar, Fine Motor, Gross Motor, Respiratory).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Revised Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis Functional Rating Scale (ALSFRS-R) is the gold standard clinical instrument for monitoring functional decline in motor neuron disease. Tracking the rate of decline guides multidisciplinary care, respiratory support (NIV), and discussions regarding percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy (PEG) placement.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS)
  • Motor Neuron Disease Progression

Key Medical Decision: Monitors disease progression rate, timing of non-invasive ventilation, and nutritional support.

ALSFRS-R ScoreFunctional Status
36 – 48 PointsMild-to-moderate functional impairment; largely independent
20 – 35 PointsModerate impairment; assistance required for mobility and ADLs
< 20 PointsAdvanced motor neuron disease; profound bulbar and respiratory compromise
4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects progressive degeneration of upper and lower motor neurons in the motor cortex, brainstem, and spinal cord.
