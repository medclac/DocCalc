Alvarado Score (MANTRELS) for Acute Appendicitis

Calculation ID: alvaradoScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 8 clinical parameters (Symptoms, Signs, and Laboratory counts; max 10)
Normal Range / Target: Score 1 – 4 (Low Probability)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Symptoms: Migration, Anorexia, Nausea/vomiting.
  • Signs: RLQ tenderness, Rebound tenderness, Elevated temperature.
  • Labs: Leukocytosis, Neutrophil left shift.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Alvarado score (MANTRELS) provides a practical clinical scoring system to stratify the probability of acute appendicitis in patients presenting with right lower quadrant abdominal pain. By standardizing symptoms, physical exam findings, and leukocytosis, it assists emergency physicians and surgeons in deciding between immediate surgical exploration, active clinical observation, or diagnostic cross-sectional imaging.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Right Lower Quadrant Abdominal Pain
  • Emergency Triage for Acute Appendicitis

Key Medical Decision: Guides decision-making between early discharge, diagnostic imaging (ultrasound/CT), and urgent surgical consultation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects progressive luminal obstruction, wall distension, localized inflammation, and systemic leukocytic response in the vermiform appendix.
