Amniotic Fluid Assessment (AFI & SDP / MVP)

Calculation ID: amnioticFluidAssessment
1. Formula & Parameters
AFI (sum of 4 quadrants) and Single Deepest Pocket (SDP / MVP)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates amniotic fluid volume via Amniotic Fluid Index (AFI) and Single Deepest Pocket (SDP).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Quantifying amniotic fluid volume via ultrasound is essential for evaluating fetal well-being, diagnosing oligohydramnios (associated with uteroplacental insufficiency or renal anomalies), and polyhydramnios (associated with fetal anomalies or gestational diabetes). SMFM guidelines recommend SDP as the preferred metric to avoid overdiagnosis of oligohydramnios.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Third Trimester Growth Ultrasound
  • Post-Term Pregnancy Surveillance

Key Medical Decision: Triggers closer feto-placental surveillance, Doppler studies, or delivery planning when abnormal volume is detected.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects the balance between fetal urine production, swallowing, and intramembranous absorption.
