APACHE II Mortality Score

Calculation ID: apache2Score
1. Formula & Parameters
Score = Acute Physiology Score (0-60) + Age Points (0-6) + Chronic Health Points (2-5)
Normal Range / Target: Score 0–9 = Low severity (~3%–5% mortality)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Acute Physiology Score: Sum of 12 worst physiological variables (0-60 pts).
  • Age Points: 0 to 6 pts based on age bracket.
  • Chronic Health Points: 2 to 5 pts for severe organ insufficiency.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Benchmarks ICU severity of illness, predicts hospital mortality, and facilitates risk adjustment in clinical research.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Critical Illness
  • ICU Admission Risk Stratification

Key Medical Decision: Provides objective prognostic data for family conferences and critical care resource allocation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Physiological derangements across multiple organ systems reflect the depth of critical illness and systemic physiological stress.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Developed by Knaus, Draper, Wagner, and Zimmerman (Crit Care Med, 1985).
