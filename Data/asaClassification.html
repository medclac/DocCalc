ASA Physical Status Classification System

Calculation ID: asaClassification
1. Formula & Parameters
6-tier physiological fitness grading system (ASA I to VI) with 'E' emergency modifier
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Graded from normal healthy (I) to moribund (V) and brain-dead organ donor (VI).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The ASA Physical Status classification is the universal preoperative assessment grading system used by anesthesiologists and surgeons to evaluate baseline patient physiological fitness and perioperative risk.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Preoperative Anesthetic & Surgical Risk Evaluation

Key Medical Decision: Stratifies baseline operative risk and informs anesthesia planning and ICU resource utilization.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects overall somatic health and systemic organ reserve under anesthetic and surgical stress.
