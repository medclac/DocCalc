ASAS Classification Criteria for Axial Spondyloarthritis

Calculation ID: asas_axspa
1. Formula & Parameters
Imaging arm (Sacroiliitis + ≥1 SpA feature) OR Clinical arm (HLA-B27 + ≥2 SpA features)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Entry: Back pain ≥3m, onset <45y. Imaging Arm: Sacroiliitis + 1 SpA feature. Clinical Arm: HLA-B27 + 2 SpA features.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The ASAS classification criteria for axial spondyloarthritis enable early identification of both radiographic ankylosing spondylitis and non-radiographic axial SpA. Early classification prevents diagnostic delay and permits timely initiation of targeted biologic therapy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Identifies early inflammatory enthesitis and sacroiliitis characteristic of the spondyloarthritides.
