AVPU Consciousness Scale

Calculation ID: avpuScale
1. Formula & Parameters
Categorical triage scale: Alert, Voice, Pain, Unresponsive
Normal Range / Target: Alert (A)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • A = Alert, V = Responds to Voice, P = Responds to Pain, U = Unresponsive.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The AVPU scale provides a rapid, streamlined first-responder screening tool for assessing consciousness in emergency triage. By categorizing patients into four distinct tiers, prehospital providers and emergency staff can instantly recognize severe neurological depression that demands airway intervention. Although less granular than the GCS, AVPU correlates strongly with clinical deterioration and underpins rapid trauma and medical resuscitation algorithms.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Emergency Triage & Prehospital Care
  • Acute Altered Mental Status
  • Trauma Resuscitation

Key Medical Decision: Triggers rapid airway assessment, high-flow oxygen administration, and preparation for endotracheal intubation when patients score 'Pain' or 'Unresponsive'.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects global cerebral hemispheric depression or brainstem reticular activating system failure leading to reduced environmental responsiveness.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Validation of the AVPU scale in emergency triage (Kelly CA, Upex A, Bateman DN. Emerg Med J. 2005;22(4):276-279).
