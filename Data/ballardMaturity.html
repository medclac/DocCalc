New Ballard Score for Neonatal Gestational Maturity

Calculation ID: ballardMaturity
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 6 neuromuscular and 6 physical criteria converted to gestational age in weeks
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates neuromuscular tone and physical characteristics to estimate gestational age from 20 to 44 weeks.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The New Ballard Score is a validated clinical tool used to estimate the gestational age of newborn infants based on neuromuscular and physical maturity characteristics. It is especially valuable when antenatal dating is uncertain or unavailable, guiding appropriate neonatal care and nutritional support.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Neonatal Gestational Age Assessment
  • Prematurity Staging in Delivery Suite

Key Medical Decision: Guides neonatal intensive care admission criteria and feeding protocols based on physiological maturity.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects the sequential maturation of central nervous system tone and cutaneous/integumentary physical development.
