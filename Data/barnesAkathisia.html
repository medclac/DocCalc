Barnes Akathisia Rating Scale (BARS)

Calculation ID: barnesAkathisia
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of Objective restlessness + Subjective restlessness + Global clinical assessment
Normal Range / Target: 0 – 1 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Assesses drug-induced subjective restlessness and observable motor pacing/fidgeting.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Barnes Akathisia Rating Scale (BARS) assesses neuroleptic-induced akathisia—a distressing sensation of inner motor restlessness accompanied by compulsive movement. A global score of 2 or higher confirms akathisia, preventing misdiagnosis as psychotic agitation and ensuring appropriate treatment (e.g., beta-blockers).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Neuroleptic-Induced Akathisia

Key Medical Decision: Prompts initiation of beta-blocker therapy (propranolol) or reduction of offending antipsychotic agent.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Dysregulation of mesocortical and nigrostriatal dopaminergic and noradrenergic pathways.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Barnes TR. (Br J Psychiatry. 1989;154:672-676).
