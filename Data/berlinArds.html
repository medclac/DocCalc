Berlin Definition of ARDS

Calculation ID: berlinArds
1. Formula & Parameters
PaO2 / FiO2 Ratio with PEEP ≥ 5 cmH2O (Mild: 201-300, Mod: 101-200, Severe: ≤100)
Normal Range / Target: P/F Ratio > 300 mmHg

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Timing: Within 1 week of known clinical insult.
  • Imaging: Bilateral opacities not fully explained by effusions.
  • Origin: Respiratory failure not fully explained by cardiac failure.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Standardizes ARDS diagnosis and severity stratification, directly correlating with mortality and lung-protective ventilation protocols.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS)
  • Severe Pneumonia
  • Sepsis / Aspiration Pneumonitis

Key Medical Decision: Guides low tidal volume ventilation (6 mL/kg), higher PEEP titration, neuromuscular blockade, prone positioning, and ECMO evaluation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Diffuse alveolar damage (DAD) leads to increased alveolar-capillary permeability, proteinaceous edema fluid accumulation, loss of aerated lung tissue, and severe intrapulmonary shunting.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Formulated by the ARDS Definition Task Force (JAMA, 2012).
