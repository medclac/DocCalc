Berlin Definition of ARDS

Calculation ID: berlinArds
1. Formula & Parameters
Timing, Imaging, Edema origin, and PaO₂/FiO₂ ratio with PEEP ≥5 cm H₂O
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates acute onset ≤7 days, bilateral opacities, non-cardiogenic origin, PEEP ≥5, and P/F ratio staging (Mild 201-300, Mod 101-200, Sev ≤100).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Berlin definition is the international consensus standard for diagnosing and grading Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS). Staging hypoxemia severity via PaO₂/FiO₂ ratio guides lung-protective ventilation, prone positioning, neuromuscular blockade, and ECMO evaluation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS) Diagnosis & Staging
  • Critical Care Mechanical Ventilation Management

Key Medical Decision: Guides initiation of lung-protective ventilation, PEEP titration, prone positioning, and ECMO consideration.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Diffuse alveolar damage, increased alveolar-capillary permeability, non-cardiogenic pulmonary edema, and severe intrapulmonary shunting.
