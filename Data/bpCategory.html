Blood Pressure Category Classifier

Calculation ID: bpCategory
1. Formula & Parameters
Classification based on SBP and DBP thresholds
Normal Range / Target: Normal: < 120 / < 80 mmHg

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Normal: <120/<80
  • Elevated: 120–129/<80
  • Stage 1: 130–139/80–89
  • Stage 2: ≥140/≥90
  • Crisis: >180/>120
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Stratifies cardiovascular risk and guides initiation of pharmacological and non-pharmacological blood pressure lowering.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Essential Hypertension
  • Hypertensive Urgency / Emergency

Key Medical Decision: Determines lifestyle intervention versus antihypertensive drug initiation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Elevated systemic vascular resistance and arterial stiffness increase myocardial afterload and vascular end-organ injury.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

ACC/AHA 2017 Guidelines (Whelton et al., J Am Coll Cardiol, 2018).
