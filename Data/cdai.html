CDAI (Clinical Disease Activity Index)

Calculation ID: cdai
1. Formula & Parameters
TJC28 + SJC28 + Patient Global (0–10) + Physician Global (0–10)
Normal Range / Target: ≤ 2.8 (Remission)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • TJC28 & SJC28: Joint counts.
  • Patient & Physician Global: 0 to 10 visual scales.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Clinical Disease Activity Index (CDAI) is a point-of-care tool for rheumatoid arthritis that eliminates the need for laboratory acute-phase reactants. By summing joint counts and global assessments directly during the clinical encounter, it enables immediate treat-to-target decision-making without waiting for blood test results.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Point-of-Care Rheumatoid Arthritis Triage

Key Medical Decision: Enables immediate prescription adjustments and shared decision-making during clinic visits.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects the direct clinical summation of peripheral synovial tenderness, swelling, and overall patient/physician disease severity estimation.
