CHA₂DS₂-VASc (AF Cardioembolic Stroke Risk)

Calculation ID: cha2ds2vascRef
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of stroke risk factors in non-valvular atrial fibrillation
Normal Range / Target: 0 (Male) | 1 (Female)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores CHF, Hypertension, Age, Diabetes, Stroke history (2 pts), Vascular disease, and Female sex.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The CHA₂DS₂-VASc score is the international gold standard for stratifying cardioembolic stroke risk in non-valvular atrial fibrillation. It determines the necessity and urgency of oral anticoagulation therapy to prevent devastating cardioembolic strokes, establishing clear risk thresholds favoring Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Non-Valvular Atrial Fibrillation
  • Cardioembolic Stroke Prevention

Key Medical Decision: Indicates initiation of oral anticoagulation (Apixaban, Rivaroxaban, Dabigatran, Edoxaban).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Left atrial blood stasis and endothelial stasis promote thrombus formation in the left atrial appendage, leading to cardioembolic occlusion of cerebral arteries.
