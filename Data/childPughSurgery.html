Child–Pugh Score for Liver Surgical Risk

Calculation ID: childPughSurgery
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 5 clinical/laboratory variables (Bilirubin, Albumin, INR, Ascites, Encephalopathy; 5–15)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Variables: Total bilirubin, serum albumin, INR, ascites severity, and hepatic encephalopathy grade.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Child-Pugh score grades the physiological severity of liver cirrhosis to estimate perioperative mortality and surgical risk prior to abdominal operations. Class A carries low risk, whereas Class C is a strong contraindication to elective abdominal surgery.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Cirrhosis Surgical Risk Assessment

Key Medical Decision: Determines surgical candidacy and perioperative mortality risk in patients with chronic liver disease.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures synthetic liver function, portal hypertension severity, and hepatic metabolic reserve.
