Clavien–Dindo Classification of Surgical Complications

Calculation ID: clavienDindo
1. Formula & Parameters
Grading postoperative morbidity based on the therapeutic intervention required to treat the complication
Normal Range / Target: Grade 0 (No Complication)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades I–II: Bedside care / pharmacological treatment.
  • Grades IIIa–IIIb: Interventions without/with general anesthesia.
  • Grades IVa–IVb: ICU single/multiorgan failure.
  • Grade V: Mortality.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Clavien-Dindo classification is the universal standard for reporting postoperative surgical complications. By grading complications according to the invasiveness of therapy required to manage them, it provides an objective, reproducible metric for surgical audits, quality improvement, and clinical trials.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Postoperative Surgical Audit & Quality Improvement
  • Morbidity & Mortality (M&M) Reporting

Key Medical Decision: Standardizes complication reporting across surgical specialties and clinical trials.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Gradates the severity of physiological deviation from an ideal postoperative course requiring therapeutic remediation.
