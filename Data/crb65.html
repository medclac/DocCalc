CRB-65 Pneumonia Severity Score

Calculation ID: crb65
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 4 criteria: Confusion, RR ≥30, BP criteria, Age ≥65 (Urea-free)
Normal Range / Target: 0 points (Low Risk)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Confusion, RR ≥30, BP criteria, Age ≥65: 1 point each.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The CRB-65 score is a urea-free bedside risk stratification tool for community-acquired pneumonia. Designed for primary care, emergency triage, or prehospital environments where blood tests are unavailable, it provides rapid mortality risk assessment to guide immediate hospital referral.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Primary Care & Prehospital Pneumonia Triage

Key Medical Decision: Determines urgent hospital referral and admission requirements before laboratory blood results are finalized.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Evaluates vital sign instability, advanced age, and acute neurological disturbance resulting from pulmonary infection.
