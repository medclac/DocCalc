Intrapartum Cardiotocography (FIGO / NICHD 3-Tier)

Calculation ID: ctgInterpretation
1. Formula & Parameters
Systematic 3-tier FHR classification (Baseline, Variability, Decelerations)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Classifies FHR into Category I (normal), Category II (indeterminate), and Category III (pathological).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Intrapartum cardiotocography (CTG) monitoring evaluates fetal oxygenation and acid-base status during labor. The 3-tier FIGO/NICHD classification framework provides clear management pathways—from routine reassurance for Category I to urgent intrauterine resuscitation or operative delivery for Category III pathological patterns.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Intrapartum Fetal Surveillance
  • Labor Suite Fetal Distress Triage

Key Medical Decision: Guides conservative intrauterine resuscitation maneuvers versus emergency operative delivery for non-reassuring traces.

Category I (Normal / Reassuring)Normal acid-base balance; well-oxygenated fetal nervous systemNegligible risk of acidemiaRoutine intrapartum care; continue electronic fetal monitoring without resuscitation
Category II (Indeterminate)Not predictive of abnormal fetal acid-base status; requires continued surveillanceLow-to-moderate risk of emerging acidemiaInitiate conservative intrauterine resuscitation (left lateral position, IV fluid bolus, stop oxytocin, check maternal vitals); reassess
Category III (Pathological / Abnormal)Predictive of abnormal fetal acid-base status, hypoxia, and cerebral acidemiaExtremely high risk of severe asphyxia / metabolic acidosisURGENT RESUSCITATION: Stop oxytocin, give tocolysis (terbutaline), oxygen; prepare for immediate operative delivery if unresolved within 10–15 min
4. Pathophysiological Background

Fetal heart rate autonomic reflex control via sympathetic and parasympathetic pathways in response to tissue oxygenation.

5. Medical Background & Evidence
