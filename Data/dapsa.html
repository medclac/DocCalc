DAPSA (Disease Activity in PsA)

Calculation ID: dapsa
1. Formula & Parameters
TJC68 + SJC66 + Patient Pain (0–10) + Patient Global (0–10) + CRP (mg/dL)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • TJC68 & SJC66: Peripheral joint counts.
  • Pain & Global: 0–10 VAS scales.
  • CRP: mg/dL.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Disease Activity in Psoriatic Arthritis (DAPSA) is a validated continuous composite index evaluating peripheral joint disease in PsA. It guides treat-to-target management strategies, targeting remission (≤4) or low disease activity (≤14).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Psoriatic Arthritis Disease Activity Monitoring

Key Medical Decision: Guides therapeutic decisions and DMARD/biologic escalation in psoriatic arthritis.

DAPSA ScoreDisease Activity StateTherapeutic TargetManagement
≤ 4.0RemissionPrimary TargetOptimal peripheral disease control
4.1 – 14.0Low Disease ActivityAcceptable TargetContinue current therapy with periodic evaluation
14.1 – 28.0Moderate ActivitySuboptimalIntensify DMARD or initiate targeted biologic therapy
> 28.0High Disease ActivityUncontrolled DiseasePrompt escalation: switch or add biologic/targeted agent
4. Pathophysiological Background

Sums peripheral joint synovitis counts, patient pain/global scoring, and systemic C-reactive protein levels.

5. Medical Background & Evidence
