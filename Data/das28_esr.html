DAS28-ESR (Disease Activity Score 28 with ESR)

Calculation ID: das28_esr
1. Formula & Parameters
0.56 × √(TJC) + 0.28 × √(SJC) + 0.70 × ln(ESR) + 0.014 × PatientGlobal
Normal Range / Target: < 2.6 (Clinical Remission)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • TJC: Tender Joint Count (0–28).
  • SJC: Swollen Joint Count (0–28).
  • ESR: Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (mm/hr).
  • Patient Global Health: Visual Analogue Scale (0–100 mm).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Disease Activity Score 28 using Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate (DAS28-ESR) is the premier composite measure utilized globally to quantify disease activity in rheumatoid arthritis (RA). By integrating counts of tender and swollen joints across 28 specific anatomical sites with acute-phase reactants (ESR) and patient global assessment, DAS28 provides an objective continuous score. This score guides treat-to-target clinical strategies, determining whether to maintain, de-escalate, or aggressively intensify synthetic or biologic disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Rheumatoid Arthritis Disease Activity Monitoring
  • Treat-to-Target Therapeutic Optimization
  • Clinical Trial Endpoints for Biologic/Targeted DMARDs

Key Medical Decision: Directs treatment intensification when scores indicate moderate or high disease activity (>3.2) or confirms sustained remission (<2.6) for potential DMARD tapering.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Synthesizes systemic inflammatory burden (ESR), subjective patient-reported systemic well-being (Global VAS), and local synovial macrophage/cytokine-driven joint inflammation (tender and swollen joint counts).
