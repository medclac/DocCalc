Expanded Disability Status Scale (EDSS)

Calculation ID: edssScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Kurtzke functional systems impairment and ambulation milestone rating (0 to 10)
Normal Range / Target: 0.0

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates functional systems (pyramidal, cerebellar, brainstem, sensory, bowel/bladder, visual, cerebral).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Expanded Disability Status Scale (EDSS) is the benchmark clinical instrument for quantifying neurological disability and disease progression in multiple sclerosis. It spans from 0.0 (normal) to 10.0 (death due to MS), guiding disease-modifying therapy selection and measuring long-term therapeutic efficacy in clinical trials.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Multiple Sclerosis Disability Staging

Key Medical Decision: Guides selection of disease-modifying therapies (DMTs) and tracks therapeutic response or disability accumulation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures cumulative axonal loss and demyelination across multiple central nervous system white matter tracts.
