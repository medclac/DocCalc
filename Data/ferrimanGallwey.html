Modified Ferriman-Gallwey (mFG) Score for Hirsutism

Calculation ID: ferrimanGallwey
1. Formula & Parameters
Visual scoring of terminal hair growth across 9 body regions (0 to 36)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores 9 androgen-sensitive body areas from 0 (none) to 4 (extensive).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Modified Ferriman-Gallwey (mFG) scale is the clinical standard for quantifying terminal hair growth and diagnosing hirsutism. It assists endocrinologists and gynecologists in evaluating hyperandrogenic states such as Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) and monitoring response to anti-androgen therapy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Hirsutism & Clinical Hyperandrogenism
  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)

Key Medical Decision: Establishes clinical hirsutism severity and guides anti-androgen or cosmetic pharmacotherapy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Evaluates end-organ pilosebaceous unit sensitivity and cutaneous 5-alpha-reductase activity stimulated by circulating androgens.
