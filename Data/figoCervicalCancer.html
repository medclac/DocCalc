2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2018 revised FIGO staging system for cervical cancer incorporates advanced imaging (MRI/PET-CT) and pathological lymph node assessment alongside clinical examination. Accurate staging dictates whether treatment should involve fertility-sparing surgery, radical hysterectomy, or definitive concurrent chemoradiotherapy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

4. Pathophysiological Background

Spread of squamous cell carcinoma or adenocarcinoma from the exocervix/endocervix into local pelvic structures and lymphatics.
