Modified Fisher Scale for SAH Vasospasm

Calculation ID: fisherScale
1. Formula & Parameters
CT-based grading of cisternal SAH and intraventricular hemorrhage
Normal Range / Target: Grade 1 (Thin SAH, no IVH)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades 1 to 4 based on cisternal blood thickness and bilateral intraventricular hemorrhage.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Modified Fisher Scale uses non-contrast head CT findings to predict the risk of delayed cerebral ischemia (DCI) and symptomatic vasospasm following subarachnoid hemorrhage. High grades (Grade 3 and 4) denote dense subarachnoid blood clots in basal cisterns, prompting aggressive prophylactic therapy with oral nimodipine and heightened transcranial Doppler (TCD) surveillance.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Aneurysmal Subarachnoid Hemorrhage
  • Delayed Cerebral Ischemia (DCI) Surveillance

Key Medical Decision: Directs neuro-ICU vasospasm monitoring protocols and induced hypertension strategies.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Degradation of subarachnoid blood clots releases oxyhemoglobin into CSF, triggering endothelial injury, smooth muscle contraction, and vasospasm.
