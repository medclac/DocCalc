FOUR Coma Score (Full Outline of UnResponsiveness)

Calculation ID: fourScore
1. Formula & Parameters
FOUR Score = Eye (0–4) + Motor (0–4) + Brainstem (0–4) + Respiration (0–4)
Normal Range / Target: 16 (Fully Alert & Intact)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Eye, Motor, Brainstem, Respiration: 0 to 4 points each (Total 0–16).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The FOUR score was developed to overcome key limitations of the Glasgow Coma Scale—most notably its inability to evaluate verbal responses in endotracheally intubated patients. By incorporating detailed brainstem reflexes (pupillary and corneal pathways) and respiratory pattern analysis, the FOUR score provides a more granular assessment of brainstem integrity and prognosis. It is particularly valuable in intensive care units, where intubation, sedation, and neuromuscular blockade often obscure standard GCS verbal assessments, helping clinicians predict mortality and detect early signs of transtentorial herniation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Neurocritical Care & Mechanical Ventilation
  • Prognostication in Anoxic Brain Injury / Coma
  • Evaluation of Brain Death

Key Medical Decision: Guides neurological prognosis, directs escalation of intracranial pressure management, and supports formal brain death examination protocols.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Evaluates the functional continuity of cortical pathways (motor/eye responses), midbrain/pontine reflex arcs (pupillary/corneal), and medullary respiratory control centers.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Seminal validation study by Wijdicks EF, Bamlet WR, Maramattom BV, et al. (Ann Neurol. 2005;58(4):585-593).
