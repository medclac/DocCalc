2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Grobman nomogram provides an evidence-based, individualized probability estimate for successful vaginal birth after one prior low-transverse cesarean delivery (TOLAC). Accurate prediction helps clinicians and patients engage in informed shared decision-making, balancing the benefits of successful VBAC against the rare risk of uterine rupture.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Trial of Labor After Cesarean (TOLAC) Counselling

Key Medical Decision: Guides shared decision-making between trial of labor after cesarean (TOLAC) and elective repeat cesarean delivery (ERCS).
