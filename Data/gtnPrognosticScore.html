2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The FIGO/WHO prognostic scoring system stratifies Gestational Trophoblastic Neoplasia (GTN) into low-risk (score ≤6) and high-risk (score ≥7) categories. This distinction is life-saving: low-risk GTN is cured with single-agent chemotherapy (>99% survival), whereas high-risk GTN requires multi-agent chemotherapy regimens (EMA-CO).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Gestational Trophoblastic Neoplasia (GTN)
  • Choriocarcinoma / Invasive Moles

4. Pathophysiological Background

Malignant proliferation of placental trophoblastic tissue characterized by high vascularity and extreme sensitivity to cytotoxic chemotherapy.
