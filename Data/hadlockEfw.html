Estimated Fetal Weight (Hadlock 4-Parameter)

Calculation ID: hadlockEfw
1. Formula & Parameters
Log10(EFW) based on BPD, HC, AC, and FL measurements
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Combines Biparietal Diameter (BPD), Head Circumference (HC), Abdominal Circumference (AC), and Femur Length (FL).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Fetal weight estimation via ultrasound biometry is crucial for identifying fetal growth restriction (FGR) and macrosomia. Accurate Hadlock 4-parameter calculations guide antenatal surveillance frequency, timing of delivery, and management of suspected macrosomia or growth restriction.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Fetal Growth Restriction (FGR)
  • Suspected Macrosomia / Large for Gestational Age

Key Medical Decision: Informs antenatal testing frequency, Doppler surveillance, and delivery planning.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Mathematical regression modeling correlating multi-planar ultrasound fetal dimensions with actual birth weight.
