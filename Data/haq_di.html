HAQ-DI (Health Assessment Questionnaire Disability Index)

Calculation ID: haq_di
1. Formula & Parameters
Mean score across 8 daily functional activity categories (0.0 to 3.0)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates dressing, arising, eating, walking, hygiene, reach, grip, and complex activities (0 to 3 each).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Health Assessment Questionnaire Disability Index (HAQ-DI) is the standard patient-reported instrument measuring functional physical disability in inflammatory arthritis. Tracking HAQ scores evaluates physical impairment, therapeutic response, and long-term functional prognosis in conditions like rheumatoid arthritis.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Functional Disability Assessment in Inflammatory Arthritis

Key Medical Decision: Quantifies physical functional limitation and evaluates response to disease-modifying therapy.

HAQ-DI RangeFunctional Impairment TierClinical Interpretation
0.0 – 1.0Mild DisabilityIndependent in basic self-care; mild limitation in demanding activities
1.1 – 2.0Moderate DisabilitySignificant daily functional limitations; occupational therapy review
2.1 – 3.0Severe DisabilityMarked impairment in physical function; requires assistive devices or caregiver support
4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures the cumulative impact of joint destruction, pain, and deformity on independence in activities of daily living.
