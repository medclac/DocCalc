HEART Score for Chest Pain

Calculation ID: heartScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Score = History (0-2) + ECG (0-2) + Age (0-2) + Risk Factors (0-2) + Troponin (0-2)
Normal Range / Target: Score 0–3 = Low 6-week MACE risk (0.9%–1.7%)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • History: Suspicion for ischemia (0-2 pts).
  • ECG: Normal to significant ST depression (0-2 pts).
  • Age: <45 (0), 45-64 (1), ≥65 (2).
  • Risk Factors: Cardiovascular risk factors or prior CAD (0-2 pts).
  • Troponin: Normal to >3x normal limit (0-2 pts).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Enables safe, early discharge of low-risk chest pain patients while identifying high-risk individuals requiring invasive coronary angiography.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)
  • Stable Angina
  • Non-Cardiac Chest Pain

Key Medical Decision: Determines outpatient discharge, observation unit serial troponins, or immediate inpatient cardiology admission.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Atherosclerotic plaque disruption, coronary vasospasm, or supply-demand mismatch leads to myocardial ischemia and biomarker release.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Developed and validated by Backus, Six, Kelder et al. (Int J Cardiol, 2013).
