Modified Hinchey Classification for Acute Diverticulitis

Calculation ID: hincheyDiverticulitis
1. Formula & Parameters
CT-based anatomical staging of complicated diverticulitis (Stage 0 to IV)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Classifies diverticulitis severity from mild wall thickening (Stage 0) to generalized feculent peritonitis (Stage IV).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Modified Hinchey classification standardizes the anatomical staging of acute complicated diverticulitis based on CT findings. It dictates whether management should consist of conservative antibiotic therapy, percutaneous CT-guided abscess drainage, or emergency laparotomy and bowel resection.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Complicated Diverticulitis

Key Medical Decision: Guides treatment selection between antibiotics, percutaneous drainage, and emergency surgery (Hartmann procedure vs primary anastomosis).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Micro- or macro-perforation of colonic diverticula resulting in localized pericolic inflammation, abscess formation, or generalized peritonitis.
