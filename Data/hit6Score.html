HIT-6 (Headache Impact Test)

Calculation ID: hit6Score
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 6 functional impact items (scores 36 to 78)
Normal Range / Target: ≤ 49 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Assesses pain severity, social limitation, need to lie down, fatigue, irritability, and concentration.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Headache Impact Test (HIT-6) is a validated psychometric tool assessing the impact of chronic and episodic headaches on daily life. A score of 60 or higher denotes severe impact, signaling substantial impairment in occupational, social, and psychological well-being that warrants aggressive treatment escalation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Chronic Migraine & Headache Disorders

Key Medical Decision: Directs selection of advanced preventive therapies (e.g., CGRP monoclonal antibodies, Botox).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Captures the biopsychosocial burden of recurrent trigeminovascular pain signaling.
