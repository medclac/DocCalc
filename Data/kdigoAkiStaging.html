KDIGO AKI Staging & AEIOU Dialysis

Calculation ID: kdigoAkiStaging
1. Formula & Parameters
Stages 1–3 based on serum creatinine rise and hourly urine output criteria
Normal Range / Target: Creatinine < 1.2 mg/dL | Urine output ≥ 0.5 mL/kg/h

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Stage 1: Cr 1.5–1.9× baseline or +0.3 mg/dL.
  • Stage 2: Cr 2.0–2.9× baseline.
  • Stage 3: Cr ≥3.0× baseline or RRT initiation.
  • AEIOU: Acidosis, Electrolytes, Ingestions, Overload, Uremia.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Standardizes acute kidney injury diagnosis, tracks nephrotoxic exposure, and identifies life-threatening metabolic emergencies requiring renal replacement therapy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)
  • Sepsis-Induced Nephropathy
  • Rhabdomyolysis
  • Refractory Hyperkalemia

Key Medical Decision: Guides nephrology consultation, avoidance of nephrotoxins, drug dosing adjustments, and emergent hemodialysis/CRRT activation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Ischemic or nephrotoxic tubular injury causes tubular cell death, cast obstruction, backleak of glomerular filtrate, and precipitous decline in glomerular filtration rate (GFR).

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Published by the KDIGO Work Group (Kidney Int Suppl, 2012).
