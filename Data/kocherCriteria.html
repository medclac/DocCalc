Kocher Criteria for Pediatric Septic Arthritis

Calculation ID: kocherCriteria
1. Formula & Parameters
Score = NWB (1) + Temp >38.5°C (1) + ESR >40 (1) + WBC >12k (1)
Normal Range / Target: Score 0 = <0.2% probability of septic arthritis

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Non-weightbearing on affected limb (1 pt).
  • Fever >38.5°C (1 pt).
  • ESR >40 mm/hr (1 pt).
  • WBC >12,000 cells/µL (1 pt).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Differentiates septic arthritis of the hip from benign transient synovitis, preventing permanent joint destruction and avascular necrosis.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Septic Arthritis of the Hip
  • Transient Synovitis
  • Osteomyelitis
  • Slipped Capital Femoral Epiphysis (SCFE)

Key Medical Decision: Determines need for urgent ultrasound-guided joint aspiration and emergent surgical arthrotomy versus conservative NSAID management.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Bacterial seeding of the synovial space triggers purulent exudate accumulation, elevating intra-articular pressure, compressing epiphyseal blood vessels, and causing rapid cartilage degradation and femoral head ischemia.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Developed by Kocher, Zurakowski, and Kasser (J Bone Joint Surg Am, 1999).
