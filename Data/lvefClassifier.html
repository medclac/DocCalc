LVEF Category Classifier (Universal Definition 2021)

Calculation ID: lvefClassifier
1. Formula & Parameters
Categorizes heart failure by ejection fraction thresholds
Normal Range / Target: Normal LV systolic function (≥ 50%)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • HFrEF (≤40%), HFmrEF (41–49%), HFpEF (≥50%), HFimpEF (improved from ≤40%).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Universal classification standard for heart failure phenotypes.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Heart Failure Phenotypes

Key Medical Decision: Directs specific pharmacological pillars (e.g., four-pillar GDMT in HFrEF, SGLT2i across all categories).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Differentiates primary systolic pump failure from diastolic stiffness and preserved fractional shortening.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Bozkurt et al. (J Card Fail, 2021).
