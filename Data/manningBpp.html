Manning Biophysical Profile (BPP)

Calculation ID: manningBpp
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 5 fetal variables (NST, Breathing, Movement, Tone, Amniotic Fluid; 0–10 points)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates 5 biophysical parameters scored 2 (normal) or 0 (abnormal) each.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Manning Biophysical Profile (BPP) combines real-time ultrasonography with non-stress fetal heart rate monitoring to assess fetal well-being in high-risk pregnancies. A normal score (8–10) reliably assures fetal oxygenation and absence of acute or chronic fetal hypoxia, helping clinicians avoid unnecessary iatrogenic preterm deliveries.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • High-Risk Antenatal Fetal Surveillance
  • Fetal Growth Restriction & Post-Term Pregnancy

Key Medical Decision: Guides timing of delivery versus expectant outpatient surveillance in high-risk pregnancies.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Fetal biophysical activities are hierarchically controlled by distinct neurological centers; hypoxia selectively abolishes breathing, movement, and tone in reverse order of CNS maturation.
