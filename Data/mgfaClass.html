MGFA Clinical Classification

Calculation ID: mgfaClass
1. Formula & Parameters
Categorical staging of Myasthenia Gravis from Class I (ocular) to Class V (crisis)
Normal Range / Target: Class I

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Class I (ocular), Class II-IV (generalized mild to severe), Class V (intubation/crisis).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Myasthenia Gravis Foundation of America (MGFA) clinical classification categorizes disease severity to guide therapeutic selection and standardize research reporting. It clearly distinguishes localized ocular myasthenia from generalized and crisis states requiring intensive ventilatory support.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Myasthenia Gravis Staging & Management

Key Medical Decision: Guides selection of acute rescue therapies (IVIg/PLEX) versus chronic immunosuppression.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Progressive spread of postsynaptic neuromuscular junction blockade from extraocular to bulbar and respiratory muscles.
