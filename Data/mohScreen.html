Medication-Overuse Headache Screener

Calculation ID: mohScreen
1. Formula & Parameters
ICHD-3 criteria evaluation for medication-overuse headache
Normal Range / Target: Criteria Not Met

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Headache ≥15 days/month for >3 months with regular analgesic overuse.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Diagnoses medication-overuse headache (MOH), a disabling secondary headache disorder caused by frequent, chronic intake of acute symptomatic medications (triptans, opioids, NSAIDs). Identifying MOH is essential because preventive therapies will fail until offending analgesics are withdrawn.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Chronic Daily Headache
  • Analgesic Rebound Headache

Key Medical Decision: Guides structured medication withdrawal and transition to preventive migraine therapy.

MOH StatusClinical Recommendation
MOH ConfirmedStructured withdrawal plan, transition to preventive migraine agent
Criteria Not MetRe-evaluate headache frequency and analgesic diary
4. Pathophysiological Background

Chronic central sensitization and neuroplastic downregulation of descending pain modulatory pathways induced by frequent analgesic exposure.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Headache Classification Committee of the International Headache Society (ICHD-3. Cephalalgia. 2018;38(1):1-211).
