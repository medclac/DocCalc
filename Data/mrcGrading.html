MRC Muscle Power Grading (0–5)

Calculation ID: mrcGrading
1. Formula & Parameters
Standard 6-point manual muscle testing scale (0 to 5)
Normal Range / Target: Grade 5 (Normal Power)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • 0 = no movement; 3 = moves against gravity; 5 = normal power.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Medical Research Council (MRC) scale is the universal physical examination standard for manual muscle strength testing. It allows clinicians to grade peripheral motor weakness across individual muscle groups, facilitating precise localization of nerve root, peripheral nerve, plexus, or myopathic lesions.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Peripheral Neuropathy & Radiculopathy
  • Myopathy & Neuromuscular Disease

Key Medical Decision: Documents baseline motor strength and tracks progression or recovery of neuromuscular weakness.

MRC GradeMovement Description
Grade 0Complete paralysis
Grade 1Flicker / trace contraction only
Grade 2Movement with gravity eliminated
Grade 3Movement against gravity
Grade 4Movement against resistance
Grade 5Normal power
4. Pathophysiological Background

Evaluates the integrity of lower motor neurons, neuromuscular junctions, and skeletal muscle fibers.
