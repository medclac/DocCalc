Modified Rankin Scale (mRS)

Calculation ID: mrsScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Functional disability grading from 0 (no symptoms) to 6 (death)
Normal Range / Target: Grade 0 (No symptoms)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades 0–2 indicate independence; 3–5 indicate increasing dependency; Grade 6 is death.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Modified Rankin Scale (mRS) is the primary clinical endpoint in stroke trials and long-term neurorehabilitation. It measures degree of disability and dependence in daily activities following cerebrovascular injury. Tracking mRS over 90 days allows clinicians and researchers to evaluate true functional recovery, quality of life, and the success of acute reperfusion or neurocritical interventions.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Post-Stroke Functional Outcomes
  • Long-Term Neurorehabilitation Assessment

Key Medical Decision: Defines primary clinical success in stroke clinical trials and evaluates rehabilitation milestones.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Captures the cumulative impact of focal neurological deficits on independence in basic and instrumental activities of daily living.
