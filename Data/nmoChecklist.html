2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The International Panel for NMO Diagnosis (IPND) criteria establish rigorous diagnostic standards for Neuromyelitis Optica Spectrum Disorder (NMOSD). Accurate diagnosis is vital because standard MS disease-modifying therapies can worsen NMOSD, requiring targeted B-cell depletion or complement inhibition instead.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Neuromyelitis Optica Spectrum Disorder (NMOSD)

Key Medical Decision: Differentiates NMOSD from multiple sclerosis and directs targeted immunotherapy (e.g., Inebilizumab, Satralizumab, Eculizumab).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Pathogenic autoantibodies directed against aquaporin-4 (AQP4) water channels on astrocyte foot processes, causing complement-mediated necrosis and inflammation.
