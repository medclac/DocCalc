Orthostatic BP Change

Calculation ID: orthostaticBp
1. Formula & Parameters
ΔSBP = SBP_standing − SBP_supine | ΔDBP = DBP_standing − DBP_supine
Normal Range / Target: Drop in SBP < 20 mmHg and DBP < 10 mmHg

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Measured at 1 and 3 minutes post-standing.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Diagnoses autonomic dysfunction, intravascular volume depletion, and orthostatic intolerance.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Orthostatic Hypotension
  • Autonomic Neuropathy
  • Hypovolemia

Key Medical Decision: Promotes medication reconciliation (diuretics/vasodilators) and volume repletion.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Failure of reflex vasoconstriction and tachycardia upon assumption of upright posture, leading to cerebral hypoperfusion.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Consensus statement on orthostatic hypotension (Freeman et al., Clin Auton Res, 2011).
