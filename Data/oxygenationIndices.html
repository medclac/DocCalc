PaO₂/FiO₂ (P/F) and SpO₂/FiO₂ (S/F) Ratio

Calculation ID: oxygenationIndices
1. Formula & Parameters
P/F = PaO₂ ÷ FiO₂ | S/F = SpO₂ ÷ FiO₂
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • FiO₂: Decimal fraction (0.21–1.0).
  • PaO₂ / SpO₂: Arterial blood gas or pulse oximetry.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Oxygenation indices quantify the severity of hypoxemic respiratory failure. While the P/F ratio derived from arterial blood gas is the gold standard for ARDS classification, the non-invasive S/F ratio provides an excellent continuous surrogate using pulse oximetry, minimizing painful repeated arterial punctures.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Hypoxemic Respiratory Failure
  • ARDS Surveillance

Key Medical Decision: Assesses hypoxemia severity and tracks response to oxygen therapy and PEEP titration.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures gas exchange efficiency across the alveolar-capillary membrane under specific inspired oxygen fractions.
