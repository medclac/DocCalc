Peritoneal Cancer Index (PCI - Sugarbaker)

Calculation ID: pciCalculator
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of lesion size scores (0–3) across 13 abdominopelvic regions (max 39)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores tumor nodule size across 9 abdominopelvic regions and 4 small bowel segments (0 to 39).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Peritoneal Cancer Index (PCI) quantifies the extent and distribution of peritoneal carcinomatosis. It is vital in determining resectability for complete cytoreductive surgery (CRS) and hyperthermic intraperitoneal chemotherapy (HIPEC) in malignancies such as pseudomyxoma peritonei and colorectal cancer.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Peritoneal Carcinomatosis Evaluation
  • Cytoreductive Surgery & HIPEC Assessment

Key Medical Decision: Determines technical resectability and candidacy for cytoreductive surgery and HIPEC.
