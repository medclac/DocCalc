PESI & Simplified PESI (sPESI) for Pulmonary Embolism

Calculation ID: pesiScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of age, cancer, cardiopulmonary disease, and vital sign extremes
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores age >80, history of cancer, chronic cardiopulmonary disease, HR ≥110, SBP <100, SpO₂ <90%.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Simplified PESI (sPESI) score accurately stratifies 30-day mortality in patients with confirmed acute pulmonary embolism. A score of 0 identifies low-risk patients (mortality ~1%) who are excellent candidates for outpatient management or early hospital discharge, reducing unnecessary inpatient admissions.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Confirmed Acute Pulmonary Embolism Prognostication
  • Outpatient PE Management Selection

Key Medical Decision: Identifies low-risk PE patients eligible for outpatient treatment and early discharge.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Combines physiological vulnerability, baseline cardiopulmonary reserve, and acute hemodynamic decompensation.
