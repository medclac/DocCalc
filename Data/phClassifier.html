Pulmonary Hypertension Hemodynamic Classifier

Calculation ID: phClassifier
1. Formula & Parameters
mPAP > 20 mmHg + PAWP ≤ 15 mmHg + PVR > 2 WU
Normal Range / Target: mPAP ≤ 20 mmHg

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Resting right heart catheterization (RHC) metrics.
  • Pre-Capillary: mPAP > 20, PAWP ≤ 15, PVR > 2 WU.
  • Post-Capillary: mPAP > 20, PAWP > 15 mmHg.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Classifies pulmonary hypertension into pre-capillary, isolated post-capillary (IpcPH), or combined post- and pre-capillary (CpcPH) to dictate targeted vasodilator versus heart failure therapies.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH)
  • Left Heart Disease PH
  • Chronic Thromboembolic PH (CTEPH)

Key Medical Decision: Directs targeted pulmonary vasodilator therapy vs. aggressive left-sided volume decongestion.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Elevated pulmonary pressures result from either precapillary arteriolar remodeling/vasoconstriction or backward transmission of elevated left ventricular filling pressures.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Formulated per 2022 ESC/ERS Guidelines for the Diagnosis and Treatment of Pulmonary Hypertension (Humbert et al., Eur Heart J, 2022).
