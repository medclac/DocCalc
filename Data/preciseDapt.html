PRECISE-DAPT Score (Bleeding Risk post-PCI)

Calculation ID: preciseDapt
1. Formula & Parameters
Multivariable risk score using Hb, WBC, age, CrCl, and bleeding history
Normal Range / Target: < 25 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Score ≥ 25 favors short DAPT (3–6 months); < 25 supports standard 12-month DAPT.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Balances ischemic stent thrombosis risk against out-of-hospital bleeding risk post-PCI.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Post-PCI Antiplatelet Therapy
  • High Bleeding Risk

Key Medical Decision: Determines optimal duration of dual antiplatelet therapy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Baseline hematological and renal abnormalities heighten the hazard of antithrombotic therapy.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Costa et al. (Lancet, 2017).
