Pregnancy Dating (LMP, EDD & Gestational Age)

Calculation ID: pregnancyDating
1. Formula & Parameters
Naegele's rule adjusted for cycle length: LMP + 280 days + (cycle − 28)
Normal Range / Target: Calculated dynamically based on LMP

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • LMP: First day of Last Normal Menstrual Period.
  • Cycle Length: Average cycle length in days (default 28).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Accurate gestational dating is the cornerstone of safe antenatal care, guiding interpretation of anomaly scans, biochemical screening tests, and timing of delivery. When menstrual dating is reliable and corroborated by first-trimester ultrasound crown-rump length (CRL), it significantly minimizes post-term induction rates and iatrogenic prematurity. This tool operationalizes ACOG and international standards to compute expected delivery dates and track critical gestational milestones.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Routine Antenatal Dating
  • First and Second Trimester Obstetric Screening

4. Pathophysiological Background

Based on the biological constant of human gestation averaging 280 days (40 weeks) from the first day of the last menstrual period in a standard 28-day cycle.
