QTc Calculator (Fridericia & Bazett)

Calculation ID: qtcCalc
1. Formula & Parameters
Fridericia: QTc = QT ÷ ∛(RR) | Bazett: QTc = QT ÷ √(RR)
Normal Range / Target: < 450 ms (Men) / < 460 ms (Women)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Fridericia formula is preferred across heart rate extremes. QTc > 500 ms marks high TdP risk.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Corrects QT interval for heart rate to detect drug-induced or congenital QT prolongation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Long QT Syndrome
  • Drug-Induced QT Prolongation
  • Electrolyte Imbalance

Key Medical Decision: Promotes electrolyte repletion and discontinuation of QT-prolonging medications.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Delayed ventricular repolarization increases dispersion of refractoriness, predisposing to early afterdepolarizations and Torsades de Pointes.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Rautaharju et al. (J Am Coll Cardiol, 2009).
