2010 ACR/EULAR Rheumatoid Arthritis Classification Criteria

Calculation ID: ra_criteria
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of weighted categories: Joint involvement + Serology + Duration + Acute phase reactants
Normal Range / Target: < 6 / 10 Points (Not Classified as RA)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Joint Distribution: 0–5 pts.
  • Serology (RF/ACPA): 0–3 pts.
  • Duration: 0–1 pt.
  • Acute Phase: 0–1 pt.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2010 ACR/EULAR classification criteria enable early identification of patients presenting with inflammatory arthritis who are at high risk of developing chronic, erosive rheumatoid arthritis. Early classification permits prompt window-of-opportunity initiation of disease-modifying antirheumatic drugs to prevent permanent joint destruction.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Early Inflammatory Arthritis Classification
  • Window-of-Opportunity DMARD Initiation

Key Medical Decision: Classifies definite rheumatoid arthritis (score ≥6/10) to initiate early disease-modifying therapy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Identifies early clinical patterns of symmetrical polyarticular synovitis combined with high-titer autoantibodies (RF and ACPA).
