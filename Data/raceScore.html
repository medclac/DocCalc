RACE Score (Prehospital LVO Screen)

Calculation ID: raceScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 5 prehospital neurological items (Face, Arm, Leg, Gaze, Cortical)
Normal Range / Target: 0 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores facial palsy, arm/leg motor weakness, head/gaze deviation, and aphasia/agnosia.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Rapid Arterial oCclusion Evaluation (RACE) score is a prehospital stroke scale designed by EMS personnel to identify patients with acute large vessel occlusion (LVO). A score of 5 or higher predicts LVO with high sensitivity, allowing emergency medical services to bypass primary stroke centers and triage patients directly to comprehensive, thrombectomy-capable centers.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Prehospital Stroke Triage
  • Large Vessel Occlusion Screening

Key Medical Decision: Directs EMS routing to bypass primary stroke centers and transport directly to comprehensive stroke centers.

RACE ScoreLVO ProbabilityPrehospital EMS Action
Score 0 – 4Low (< 30%)Transport to nearest primary stroke center
Score 5 – 9High (> 70%)Bypass to EVT-capable Comprehensive Stroke Center stat
4. Pathophysiological Background

Captures severe neurological deficits resulting from proximal intracranial occlusion of the ICA or proximal MCA.
