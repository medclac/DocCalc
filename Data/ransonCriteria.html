Ranson’s Criteria for Acute Pancreatitis

Calculation ID: ransonCriteria
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 11 variables assessed at admission and at 48 hours
Normal Range / Target: 0 – 2 Points (Mild Pancreatitis)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • At Admission: Age, WBC, Glucose, AST, LDH.
  • At 48 Hours: Hct drop, BUN rise, Calcium, PaO₂, Base deficit, Fluid sequestration.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Ranson's criteria provide a classic prognostic scoring system for acute pancreatitis, assessing systemic stress and pancreatic necrosis across admission and 48-hour clinical parameters to predict morbidity and mortality.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Pancreatitis Severity Staging

Key Medical Decision: Directs level of intensive care monitoring and identifies severe necrotizing pancreatitis.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Pancreatic autodigestion leading to systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS), capillary leak, and multiorgan dysfunction.

5. Medical Background & Evidence
