RCOG Green-top VTE Risk Score (Thromboprophylaxis)

1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of antepartum and postpartum venous thromboembolism risk factors
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores prior VTE, high-risk thrombophilia, medical comorbidities, BMI, obstetric risk, and immobility.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The RCOG Green-top VTE risk assessment model standardizes antenatal and postnatal thromboprophylaxis to prevent fatal pulmonary embolism. By calculating cumulative risk points, clinicians determine exact thresholds for initiating low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH) during pregnancy and the puerperium.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Antenatal & Postnatal VTE Risk Assessment
  • Thromboprophylaxis in Pregnancy

4. Pathophysiological Background

Pregnancy induces a physiological hypercoagulable state (increased clotting factors, venous stasis, reduced fibrinolysis) compounded by endothelial risk factors.

5. Medical Background & Evidence
