Revised Cardiac Risk Index (RCRI / Lee Criteria)

Calculation ID: rcriSurgery
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of 6 clinical cardiac risk factors for non-cardiac surgery
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores high-risk surgery, ischemic heart disease, heart failure, cerebrovascular disease, insulin-dependent diabetes, and renal insufficiency (Cr >2.0).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Revised Cardiac Risk Index (RCRI) is the premier clinical prediction rule for estimating perioperative major adverse cardiac events (MACE) in non-cardiac surgery. It guides preoperative cardiac testing and optimization.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Preoperative Cardiac Risk Stratification

Key Medical Decision: Determines need for non-invasive cardiac stress testing or cardiology consultation prior to non-cardiac surgery.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Quantifies ischemic heart disease, myocardial failure, and vascular frailty under surgical stress.
