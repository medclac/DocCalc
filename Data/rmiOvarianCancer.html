Risk of Malignancy Index (RMI I) for Adnexal Masses

Calculation ID: rmiOvarianCancer
1. Formula & Parameters
RMI = Ultrasound Score (U) × Menopausal Status (M) × Serum CA-125 (U/mL)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Combines ultrasound morphology score (0–3), menopausal status (pre=1, post=3), and CA-125 level.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Risk of Malignancy Index (RMI I) is a validated clinical algorithm used to triage women with an adnexal pelvic mass. An RMI score exceeding 200 carries a 70–85% probability of ovarian malignancy, mandating direct referral to a gynecologic oncology multidisciplinary center for primary surgical staging.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pelvic Adnexal Mass Evaluation
  • Ovarian Cancer Screening

Key Medical Decision: Determines appropriate surgical referral pathway: general gynecology versus gynecologic oncology.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Integrates tissue architectural complexity (ultrasound), hormonal status (menopause), and tumor antigen secretion (CA-125).
