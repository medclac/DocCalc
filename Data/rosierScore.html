ROSIER Stroke Recognition Tool

Calculation ID: rosierScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Focal neurological signs (+1 each) minus LOC (−1) and Seizures (−1)
Normal Range / Target: Score ≤ 0

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Adds points for asymmetric weakness, visual/speech defects; subtracts for syncope and seizures.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Recognition of Stroke in the Emergency Room (ROSIER) scale helps emergency clinicians differentiate acute stroke from common stroke mimics (e.g., Todd's paresis, complicated migraine, hypoglycemia, functional neurological disorder). A score above 0 strongly supports acute stroke and expedites neuroimaging.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Emergency Department Stroke Triage
  • Differentiation of Stroke Mimics

Key Medical Decision: Prevents inappropriate thrombolysis administration in stroke mimics while expediting true stroke interventions.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Distinguishes true vascular focal deficits from generalized cerebral dysfunction (seizure/syncope).
