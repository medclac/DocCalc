Rotterdam PCOS Diagnostic Criteria

Calculation ID: rotterdamPcos
1. Formula & Parameters
Diagnosis requires ≥2 of: 1) Oligo/anovulation, 2) Hyperandrogenism, 3) PCO morphology
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates ovulatory dysfunction, clinical/biochemical hyperandrogenism, and ultrasound PCO morphology.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Rotterdam criteria provide the definitive international diagnostic framework for Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS). By recognizing multiple distinct phenotypes, clinicians can accurately diagnose PCOS, counsel patients on reproductive and metabolic risks, and initiate appropriate metabolic and hormonal management.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) Diagnosis

4. Pathophysiological Background

Complex neuroendocrine dysfunction featuring abnormal GnRH pulsatility, hyperinsulinemia, and ovarian hyperandrogenism.
