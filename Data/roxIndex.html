ROX Index for High-Flow Nasal Oxygen (HFNO)

Calculation ID: roxIndex
1. Formula & Parameters
ROX = (SpO₂ ÷ FiO₂) ÷ Respiratory Rate
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • SpO₂: Pulse oximetry %.
  • FiO₂: Fraction (0.21–1.0).
  • RR: Breaths/min.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The ROX index is a validated bedside predictor of success versus failure for patients receiving high-flow nasal cannula (HFNO) oxygen therapy for acute hypoxemic respiratory failure. Calculating ROX at 2, 6, and 12 hours helps clinicians identify failing patients early, avoiding delayed emergency intubation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Hypoxemic Respiratory Failure on HFNO
  • Pneumonia & COVID-19 Respiratory Support

Key Medical Decision: Determines timely escalation from high-flow nasal oxygen to invasive mechanical ventilation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Combines oxygenation efficiency (SpO₂/FiO₂) with ventilatory mechanics (respiratory rate) to quantify respiratory distress.
