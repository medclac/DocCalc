RVSP Estimation (Echocardiography)

Calculation ID: rvspEcho
1. Formula & Parameters
RVSP = 4 × (TR Vmax)² + RAP
Normal Range / Target: ≤ 35 mmHg

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • TR Vmax: Peak tricuspid regurgitation velocity (m/s).
  • RAP: Estimated right atrial pressure based on inferior vena cava diameter and collapsibility (3, 8, or 15 mmHg).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Non-invasive screening estimate for pulmonary arterial systolic pressure.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Suspected Pulmonary Hypertension
  • Right Ventricular Strain

Key Medical Decision: Prompts confirmatory right heart catheterization when RVSP > 50 mmHg.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Applies the modified Bernoulli equation to translate peak tricuspid regurgitation velocity into a pressure gradient between RV and RA.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Recommended by American Society of Echocardiography (ASE) Guidelines (Nagueh et al., J Am Soc Echocardiogr, 2016).
