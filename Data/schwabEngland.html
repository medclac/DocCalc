Schwab and England ADL Scale

Calculation ID: schwabEngland
1. Formula & Parameters
Percentage rating (0% to 100%) of daily living independence in Parkinson's disease
Normal Range / Target: 100%

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Percentage scale evaluating functional autonomy in daily activities.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Schwab and England Activities of Daily Living scale evaluates functional capacity and independence in patients with Parkinson's disease. It provides a quick percentage rating of how well a patient manages daily chores and self-care.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Parkinson's Disease Functional Surveillance

Key Medical Decision: Assesses caregiver burden and need for physical or occupational therapy assistance.

PercentageStatus
80% – 100%Independent daily functioning
50% – 70%Moderately dependent; requires caregiver support
≤ 40%Severely dependent to bedbound
4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures overall functional impact of motor symptoms (bradykinesia, rigidity, tremor, freezing).

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Schwab RS, England AC. (Third Symp Parkinson's Dis. 1969;152-157).
