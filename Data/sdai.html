SDAI (Simplified Disease Activity Index)

Calculation ID: sdai
1. Formula & Parameters
TJC28 + SJC28 + Patient Global (0–10) + Physician Global (0–10) + CRP (mg/dL)
Normal Range / Target: ≤ 3.3 (Stringent Remission)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Joint Counts & Globals: TJC28, SJC28, Patient Global, Physician Global.
  • CRP: mg/dL.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Simplified Disease Activity Index (SDAI) provides a robust linear mathematical composite of RA disease activity. An SDAI score ≤3.3 defines the stringent ACR/EULAR Boolean-equivalent remission standard, representing near-complete suppression of inflammatory synovitis.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Stringent ACR/EULAR Remission Assessment

Key Medical Decision: Defines strict therapeutic remission targets and guides biological/synthetic DMARD tapering strategies.
