First Seizure Recurrence Risk

Calculation ID: seizureRecurrence
1. Formula & Parameters
Stratifies risk of a second unprovoked seizure based on structural/EEG risk factors
Normal Range / Target: 0 Risk Factors

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates brain imaging abnormalities, epileptiform EEG, nocturnal timing, and focal deficit.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Stratifies the risk of seizure recurrence following a first unprovoked seizure. According to practical ILAE definitions, a single unprovoked seizure with a recurrence risk exceeding 60% over 10 years qualifies as epilepsy, guiding decisions on initiating long-term antiseizure medication.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • First Unprovoked Seizure Evaluation
  • Epilepsy Diagnosis Staging

Key Medical Decision: Establishes formal diagnosis of epilepsy and determines whether to initiate chronic antiseizure monotherapy.
