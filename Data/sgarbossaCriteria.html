Sgarbossa / Modified Sgarbossa Criteria (MI in LBBB)

Calculation ID: sgarbossaCriteria
1. Formula & Parameters
ECG diagnostic criteria for acute coronary occlusion in LBBB or paced rhythm
Normal Range / Target: Negative Sgarbossa Criteria

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Concordant STE ≥1 mm, concordant STD V1-V3, and modified discordant STE/S ratio ≤ −0.25.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Accurately diagnoses acute myocardial infarction in the presence of confounding left bundle branch block.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Left Bundle Branch Block with Suspected Acute Infarction

Key Medical Decision: Triggers immediate primary PCI catheterization laboratory activation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Primary repolarization abnormalities in LBBB are overcome by regional transmural ischemia creating vector concordance or excessive discordance.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Sgarbossa et al. (N Engl J Med, 1996); Smith Modification (Ann Emerg Med, 2012).
