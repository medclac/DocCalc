Simpson–Angus Scale (SAS)

Calculation ID: simpsonAngus
1. Formula & Parameters
Mean score of 10 items evaluating rigidity, gait, and tremor (0 to 4)
Normal Range / Target: Mean < 0.3

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates neuroleptic-induced extrapyramidal parkinsonian rigidity and hypokinesia.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Simpson–Angus Scale (SAS) evaluates drug-induced extrapyramidal parkinsonism resulting from dopamine blockade. A mean score exceeding 0.3 indicates clinically significant drug-induced parkinsonism, guiding management adjustments.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Drug-Induced Parkinsonism
  • Neuroleptic Extrapyramidal Side Effects

Key Medical Decision: Guides addition of anticholinergic medications or reduction of antipsychotic dosage.

SAS Mean ScoreInterpretation
< 0.3Within normal limits
> 0.3Extrapyramidal parkinsonian signs present
4. Pathophysiological Background

Striatal postsynaptic D2 receptor blockade disrupting nigrostriatal dopaminergic tone.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Simpson GM, Angus JW. (Acta Psychiatr Scand Suppl. 1970;212:11-19).
