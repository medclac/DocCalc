2016 ACR/EULAR Sjögren Syndrome Criteria

Calculation ID: sjogren2016
1. Formula & Parameters
Weighted sum across 5 objective domains (Anti-SSA, salivary biopsy, ocular staining, Schirmer, salivary flow)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Criteria: Anti-SSA (+3), salivary gland biopsy (+3), ocular staining score (+1), Schirmer test (+1), unstimulated salivary flow (+1).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2016 ACR/EULAR criteria classify primary Sjögren syndrome with high specificity by emphasizing objective exocrine gland dysfunction and anti-SSA seropositivity.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Primary Sjögren Syndrome Classification & Diagnosis

Key Medical Decision: Classifies primary Sjögren syndrome (score ≥4) to guide exocrine management and lymphoma surveillance.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Autoimmune lymphocytic infiltration of exocrine glands leading to glandular destruction and severe mucosal dryness.
