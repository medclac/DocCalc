SNNOOP10 Headache Red Flags

Calculation ID: snnoop10
1. Formula & Parameters
Checklist screening for secondary headache red flags
Normal Range / Target: 0 Red Flags

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Systemic, Neoplasm, Neuro deficit, Onset thunderclap, Older age, Pattern change, Positional, Papilledema.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The SNNOOP10 mnemonic provides a comprehensive clinical safety checklist to identify secondary, life-threatening etiologies of headache (e.g., subarachnoid hemorrhage, meningitis, tumor, dural venous sinus thrombosis, temporal arteritis). The presence of any red flag mandates urgent neuroimaging and diagnostic evaluation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Headache Triage
  • Secondary Headache Evaluation

Key Medical Decision: Mandates emergency neuroimaging (CT/MRI) and lumbar puncture when secondary red flags are present.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Screens for structural, vascular, infectious, or inflammatory intracranial pathology.
