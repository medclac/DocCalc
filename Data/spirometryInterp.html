Spirometry Ventilatory Pattern Interpretation

Calculation ID: spirometryInterp
1. Formula & Parameters
Stepwise analysis of Post-BD FEV₁/FVC ratio and FVC % predicted
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Step 1: Check FEV₁/FVC ratio (<0.70 = Obstruction).
  • Step 2: Check FVC (<80% = Restriction suspect).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Standardized spirometry interpretation provides the foundational differential diagnosis of pulmonary function defects. Distinguishing obstructive ventilatory defects (COPD, asthma) from possible restriction or mixed defects guides clinical pulmonology workups.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pulmonary Function Testing (PFT) Interpretation
  • Chronic Cough & Dyspnea Workup

Key Medical Decision: Categorizes ventilatory impairment into obstructive, restrictive, or mixed patterns.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures forced expiratory flows and volumes to assess airway caliber and lung volume capacities.
