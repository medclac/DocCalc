Status Epilepticus Operational Protocol (ILAE)

Calculation ID: statusEpilepticus
1. Formula & Parameters
Defines t1 (treatment initiation) and t2 (neuronal injury threshold)
Normal Range / Target: No seizure activity

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Convulsive SE: t1 = 5 minutes, t2 = 30 minutes.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The International League Against Epilepsy (ILAE) operational definition of status epilepticus establishes strict time-based treatment checkpoints. Recognizing t1 (time to initiate emergency treatment) and t2 (time beyond which ongoing seizure activity causes permanent neuronal injury and refractoriness) ensures rapid administration of first-line benzodiazepines and prevents catastrophic status epilepticus morbidity.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Generalized Convulsive Status Epilepticus
  • Focal Status Epilepticus

Key Medical Decision: Triggers immediate emergency administration of IV benzodiazepines (t1) followed by second-line antiepileptic infusions (t2).

Seizure StageOperational WindowEmergency Action
Early Seizure< t1 (0 – 5 min)Airway positioning, O2, fingerstick glucose
Established Status≥ t1 to < t2 (5 – 30 min)FIRST-LINE: IV Lorazepam (0.1 mg/kg) or IM Midazolam (10 mg) stat
Refractory Status≥ t2 (≥ 30 min)SECOND-LINE: IV Levetiracetam/Fosphenytoin; intubation + Propofol/Midazolam infusion
4. Pathophysiological Background

Failure of normal GABAergic inhibitory mechanisms and sustained excessive glutamatergic excitation resulting in excitotoxic neuronal death.
