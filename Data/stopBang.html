STOP-BANG Sleep Apnea Screening Questionnaire

Calculation ID: stopBang
1. Formula & Parameters
8-item yes/no screening score for Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • STOP: Snoring, Tiredness, Observed apnea, high blood Pressure.
  • BANG: BMI >35, Age >50, Neck >40cm, Gender male.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The STOP-BANG questionnaire is a concise, highly sensitive screening tool for obstructive sleep apnea (OSA). Widely used in preoperative clinics and outpatient settings, a high score identifies patients at severe risk for moderate-to-severe OSA, guiding perioperative airway management and formal sleep study referral.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Preoperative Airway Risk Assessment
  • Outpatient Screening for Obstructive Sleep Apnea

Key Medical Decision: Identifies patients requiring formal polysomnography and perioperative difficult airway precautions.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Screens for anatomic and physiological risk factors associated with upper airway collapse during sleep.
