TAPSE/PASP Ratio (RV-PA Coupling)

Calculation ID: tapsePasp
1. Formula & Parameters
TAPSE (mm) ÷ PASP (mmHg)
Normal Range / Target: > 0.31 mm/mmHg

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • TAPSE: Tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion (mm).
  • PASP: Estimated pulmonary arterial systolic pressure (mmHg).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Robust echocardiographic surrogate for right ventriculo-arterial coupling; predicts survival in pulmonary hypertension.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension
  • Cor Pulmonale

Key Medical Decision: Identifies high-risk RV failure and need for advanced specialist management.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Evaluates the efficiency of mechanical energy transfer from the right ventricle to the pulmonary vascular load.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Validated by Tello et al. (Circ Cardiovasc Imaging, 2019).
