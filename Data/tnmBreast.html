Breast Cancer Anatomical TNM Staging

Calculation ID: tnmBreast
1. Formula & Parameters
AJCC 8th edition stage grouping based on Tumor size (T), Lymph nodes (N), and Metastasis (M)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • T: Primary tumor size/extension. N: Regional axillary nodes. M: Distant metastasis.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The AJCC 8th edition breast cancer staging system categorizes anatomical extent to guide surgical management (lumpectomy vs mastectomy, sentinel node biopsy vs axillary dissection) and multi-modality adjuvant oncologic therapy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures primary breast tumor volume, axillary lymphatic involvement, and distant metastatic dissemination.
