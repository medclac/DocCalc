WFNS SAH Grading

Calculation ID: wfnsSah
1. Formula & Parameters
Combines GCS score with presence of major focal motor deficit
Normal Range / Target: Grade I (GCS 15, No Deficit)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades I to V based on GCS (15 to 3) and motor deficit presence.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The World Federation of Neurosurgical Societies (WFNS) grading scale provides an objective, highly reproducible classification for aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage. By relying strictly on GCS and focal motor deficits, WFNS minimizes subjective interpretation compared to older scales, offering superior prognostic accuracy for clinical trials and surgical outcomes.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Aneurysmal Subarachnoid Hemorrhage

Key Medical Decision: Guides neurosurgical management, ICU triage, and evaluation for delayed cerebral ischemia.
