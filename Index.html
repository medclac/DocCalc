How to Use on iPhone / iPad (iOS)

Since the downloadable file is strictly an Android APK, iOS users can install Docstat directly to their home screen as a standalone web app:

  • 1
    Open this website in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  • 2
    Tap the Share button (the square icon with an arrow pointing upward at the bottom of the screen).
  • 3
    Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  • 4
    Name it Docstat and tap Add in the top-right corner. It will now launch like a native app without browser bars.
Android Compatibility & Installation
System Requirement: Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher (API level 26+). Compatible with phones and tablets.
  • 1
    Tap Download Android APK above and wait for the file to finish saving.
  • 2
    Open your device's Files or Downloads manager and tap Docstat.apk.
  • 3
    If prompted with "For security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".
  • 4
    Return to the installer screen and tap Install to finish setup.
