ABCD² Score for Post-TIA Stroke Risk

Calculation ID: abcd2Score
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring: Age + BP + Clinical features + Duration + Diabetes
Normal Range / Target: Score 0 – 3 (Low Risk)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores age ≥60, BP ≥140/90, unilateral weakness/speech disturbance, duration, and diabetes.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The ABCD² score estimates early stroke risk at 2, 7, and 90 days following a transient ischemic attack (TIA). High scores (6–7) convey an ~8.1% risk of stroke within 48 hours, mandating immediate hospital admission, urgent neurovascular imaging, and rapid initiation of secondary stroke prevention therapies.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Transient Ischemic Attack (TIA)
  • Secondary Stroke Prevention

Key Medical Decision: Tries outpatient vs inpatient admission for urgent stroke workup (MRI, carotid duplex, echocardiogram).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects unstable atherosclerotic or cardioembolic plaque vulnerability causing recurrent focal ischemia.
