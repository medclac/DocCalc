Alvarado Score for Appendicitis

Calculation ID: alvaradoScore
1. Formula & Parameters
MANTRELS: Migration (1) + Anorexia (1) + Nausea/Vomiting (1) + RLQ tenderness (2) + Rebound (1) + Temp (1) + Leukocytosis (2) + Shift (1)
Normal Range / Target: Score 1–4 = Appendicitis unlikely

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Migration of pain (+1), Anorexia (+1), Nausea/Vomiting (+1).
  • Right lower quadrant tenderness (+2), Rebound tenderness (+1), Temperature elevation (+1).
  • Leukocytosis >10k (+2), Left shift (+1).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Guides diagnostic imaging and surgical consultation in emergency patients presenting with right lower quadrant abdominal pain.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Appendicitis
  • Right Lower Quadrant Abdominal Pain

Key Medical Decision: Determines outpatient discharge, diagnostic abdominal ultrasound/CT, or emergent surgical appendectomy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Obstruction of the appendiceal lumen (fecolith, lymphoid hyperplasia) leads to luminal distension, bacterial proliferation, ischemia, and transmural inflammation.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Published by Alvarado (Ann Emerg Med, 1986).
