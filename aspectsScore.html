ASPECTS (Early Ischemic Core Score)

Calculation ID: aspectsScore
1. Formula & Parameters
10-point CT topographic scoring of early ischemic changes in MCA territory
Normal Range / Target: 10 / 10 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Subtracts 1 point for each of 10 defined MCA regions showing hypoattenuation.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Alberta Stroke Program Early Computed Tomography Score (ASPECTS) is a 10-point quantitative topographic scoring system applied to non-contrast head CTs in acute MCA stroke. It grades the extent of early ischemic tissue injury. An ASPECTS score of 6 or higher confirms a small ischemic core, demonstrating that substantial salvageable penumbra remains and supporting mechanical thrombectomy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Hyperacute Ischemic Stroke
  • Large Vessel Occlusion (LVO) Selection

Key Medical Decision: Supports endovascular thrombectomy eligibility even beyond traditional 6-hour windows when advanced imaging shows favorable core.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Cytotoxic edema and early neuronal swelling causing loss of grey-white matter differentiation in specific MCA territories.
