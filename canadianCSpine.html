Canadian C-Spine Rule (CCR)

Calculation ID: canadianCSpine
1. Formula & Parameters
Screens High-risk factors (age≥65, mechanism), Low-risk factors (ROM ability), and Active 45° neck rotation
Normal Range / Target: Able to rotate neck 45° left/right = C-spine cleared

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • High-risk screening: Age ≥65, dangerous mechanism, extremity paresthesias.
  • Low-risk screening: Safe ROM assessment factors.
  • Active rotation: 45° left and right.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Extremely high sensitivity for clinically important cervical spine injury, allowing safe removal of rigid collars without radiographs.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Cervical Spine Trauma
  • Neck Pain / Whiplash

Key Medical Decision: Decides whether to remove cervical collar and clinically clear spine or obtain helical CT C-spine.

4. Pathophysiological Background

High-energy trauma or axial loading can fracture cervical vertebrae or disrupt spinal ligaments, risking spinal cord compression.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Published by Stiell, Wells, Vandemheen et al. (JAMA, 2001).
