CHA₂DS₂-VASc (AF Stroke Risk)

Calculation ID: cha2ds2vasc
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of stroke risk factors in non-valvular atrial fibrillation
Normal Range / Target: Score 0 (Male) / 1 (Female)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores CHF, HTN, Age ≥75 (2 pts), Diabetes, Stroke (2 pts), Vascular disease, Age 65–74, Female sex.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Guides oral anticoagulation therapy for stroke prevention in non-valvular atrial fibrillation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Non-Valvular Atrial Fibrillation

Key Medical Decision: Indicates initiation of direct oral anticoagulants (DOACs) or warfarin.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Left atrial stasis and endothelial dysfunction promote thrombus formation in the left atrial appendage, leading to cardioembolic stroke.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

ESC Atrial Fibrillation Guidelines (Hindricks et al., Eur Heart J, 2021).
