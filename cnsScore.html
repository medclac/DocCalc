Canadian Neurological Scale (CNS)

Calculation ID: cnsScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Mentation score (1.5–3.0) + Motor score (1.5–7.0)
Normal Range / Target: 10.0 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates mentation (level of consciousness, orientation, speech) and motor function.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Canadian Neurological Scale (CNS) is designed specifically for serial nursing and medical evaluations of non-comatose acute stroke patients. Its weighted design allows rapid detection of early neurological deterioration (e.g., stroke in evolution, hemorrhagic transformation, or cerebral edema).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Inpatient Stroke Unit Serial Monitoring

Key Medical Decision: Alerts clinical team to neurological decline, prompting repeat neuroimaging.

Total ScoreClinical State
Score 8.5 – 10.0Mild or resolving neurological deficit
Score 5.0 – 8.0Moderate neurological deficit
Score < 5.0Severe neurological impairment; alert clinical team
4. Pathophysiological Background

Tracks dynamic changes in cortical integration and motor pathway conduction.
