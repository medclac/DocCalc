EGIRIS (GBS Respiratory Failure Risk)

Calculation ID: egrisScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring: Days to admission + Bulbar weakness + Admission MRC sum score
Normal Range / Target: 0 – 2 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores time from weakness onset, bulbar weakness, and admission MRC sum score.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Erasmus GBS Respiratory Insufficiency Score (EGRIS) predicts the probability that a patient with Guillain–Barré syndrome will require mechanical ventilation within the first week of admission. High scores (5–7) identify high-risk patients (~65% ventilation probability), prompting proactive ICU admission and frequent pulmonary function testing.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Guillain–Barré Syndrome Respiratory Monitoring

Key Medical Decision: Prompts preemptive ICU triage and frequent monitoring of forced vital capacity (FVC) and negative inspiratory force (NIF).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects rapid demyelinating involvement of phrenic and intercostal motor nerve fibers.
