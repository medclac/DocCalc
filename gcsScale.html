Glasgow Coma Scale & GCS-Pupils

Calculation ID: gcsScale
1. Formula & Parameters
GCS = Eye (1-4) + Verbal (1-5) + Motor (1-6) | GCS-P = GCS − Unreactive Pupils (0-2)
Normal Range / Target: GCS 15 = Fully conscious and alert

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Eye opening response (1-4 pts).
  • Verbal response (1-5 pts).
  • Motor response (1-6 pts).
  • Pupillary reactivity adjustment (subtract 0-2 pts).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Universal standard for assessing level of consciousness and traumatic brain injury severity; GCS ≤ 8 mandates endotracheal intubation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI)
  • Stroke / Intracranial Hemorrhage
  • Acute Encephalopathy / Coma

Key Medical Decision: Determines head CT urgency, neurosurgical consultation, and need for emergency airway protection.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Diffuse axonal injury, raised intracranial pressure, or hemispheric ischemia disrupts ascending reticular activating system and cortical-subcortical pathways.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Original scale by Teasdale and Jennett (Lancet, 1974); GCS-Pupils update by Teasdale et al. (Lancet Neurol, 2014).
