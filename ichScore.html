ICH Score (Intracerebral Hemorrhage Mortality)

Calculation ID: ichScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of GCS, Age, Volume, IVH, and Infratentorial location
Normal Range / Target: Score 0 (0% 30-Day Mortality)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores GCS, age ≥80, volume ≥30 mL, IVH presence, and infratentorial location.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The ICH score is a robust, validated clinical prediction rule estimating 30-day mortality in patients with primary spontaneous intracerebral hemorrhage. By integrating clinical exam, hemorrhage volume, and anatomical location, clinicians can rapidly communicate prognosis to families and guide critical care interventions, such as intensive blood pressure lowering and surgical hematoma evacuation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Spontaneous Intracerebral Hemorrhage (ICH)
  • Neurocritical Care Prognostication

Key Medical Decision: Guides goals-of-care discussions, ICU triage, and aggressive blood pressure titration.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Combines direct mass effect, elevated intracranial pressure, intraventricular extension, and brainstem compression.
