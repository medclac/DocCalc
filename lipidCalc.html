LDL-C (Friedewald) & Non-HDL Cholesterol

Calculation ID: lipidCalc
1. Formula & Parameters
LDL-C = TC − HDL − (TG ÷ 5) | Non-HDL-C = TC − HDL
Normal Range / Target: LDL-C < 100 mg/dL

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Valid only when Triglycerides < 400 mg/dL.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Quantifies primary atherogenic lipoprotein burden (LDL and total non-HDL cholesterol).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Dyslipidemia
  • Atherosclerotic Cardiovascular Disease

Key Medical Decision: Guides lipid-lowering pharmacotherapy intensity.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Atherogenic lipoproteins penetrate the arterial intima, undergo oxidation, and drive foam cell plaque formation.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Friedewald, Levy, and Fredrickson (Clin Chem, 1972).
