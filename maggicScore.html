MAGGIC Heart Failure Mortality Risk Score

Calculation ID: maggicScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Multivariable risk score utilizing 13 demographic and clinical parameters
Normal Range / Target: < 16 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Validated across HFrEF and HFpEF populations.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Predicts 1-year and 3-year all-cause mortality in heart failure.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Heart Failure (HFrEF and HFpEF)

Key Medical Decision: Informs prognosis discussions and advanced therapy referrals.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Integrates myocardial impairment, renal failure, age, and systemic comorbidities.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Pocock et al. (Eur Heart J, 2013).
