MG-ADL (Myasthenia Gravis ADL)

Calculation ID: mgAdlScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 8 patient-reported functional limitation items (0 to 24 points)
Normal Range / Target: 0 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates talking, chewing, swallowing, breathing, grooming, arising, double vision, and ptosis.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Myasthenia Gravis Activities of Daily Living (MG-ADL) scale is a patient-reported outcome instrument tracking functional impairment in myasthenia gravis. By assessing ocular, bulbar, respiratory, and limb symptoms, the MG-ADL provides a rapid, clinically meaningful assessment of disease severity and treatment response.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Myasthenia Gravis Disease Management

Key Medical Decision: Monitors therapeutic efficacy of immunotherapy, acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, and thymectomy.

MG-ADL ScoreFunctional Impact
0 – 1 PointsMinimal or no symptom impact
2 – 5 PointsMild functional impairment
≥ 6 PointsModerate-to-severe functional limitation; treatment escalation required
4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects antibody-mediated destruction of postsynaptic nicotinic acetylcholine receptors at the neuromuscular junction.
