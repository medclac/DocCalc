Mini-Cog Cognitive Screen

Calculation ID: miniCog
1. Formula & Parameters
3-Word Recall (0–3) + Clock Drawing Test (Normal/Abnormal)
Normal Range / Target: Negative Screen

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Combines delayed recall of 3 unrelated words with a visuospatial clock drawing test.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Mini-Cog is a rapid, 3-minute cognitive screening instrument designed to detect dementia and cognitive impairment in primary care and acute settings. By combining verbal memory with executive visuospatial clock drawing, it minimizes educational and language bias while effectively flagging patients who require formal neuropsychological evaluation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Dementia Screening in Primary Care & Geriatrics
  • Cognitive Impairment Triage

Key Medical Decision: Triggers formal neuropsychological testing, brain MRI, and laboratory workup for cognitive decline.

Screen ResultInterpretation
Positive ScreenCognitive impairment / dementia likely; formal neuropsych testing indicated
Negative ScreenLow probability of significant dementia
4. Pathophysiological Background

Assesses hippocampal memory consolidation and frontal-parietal executive visuospatial processing.
