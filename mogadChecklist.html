2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2023 international consensus criteria define MOG antibody-associated disease (MOGAD) as a distinct demyelinating entity separate from MS and AQP4-positive NMOSD. Establishing this diagnosis guides acute relapse management and long-term immunosuppression strategies.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Autoantibodies targeting myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein (MOG) on the outermost surface of myelin sheaths.
