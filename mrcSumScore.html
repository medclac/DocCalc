MRC Sum Score (ICU Weakness & GBS)

Calculation ID: mrcSumScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 6 bilateral muscle group scores (0 to 60 points)
Normal Range / Target: 60 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee, and ankle muscle groups bilaterally (0–10 each).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The MRC Sum Score is a standardized quantitative bedside instrument used to diagnose ICU-acquired weakness (ICUAW) and monitor motor recovery in Guillain–Barré syndrome. A total score below 48 points confirms clinically significant ICU-acquired weakness, conveying prolonged mechanical ventilation and increased mortality.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • ICU-Acquired Weakness (Critical Illness Polyneuropathy/Myopathy)
  • Guillain–Barré Syndrome

Key Medical Decision: Establishes diagnosis of ICUAW and guides targeted physical rehabilitation protocols.

MRC Sum ScoreClinical Interpretation
Score 48 – 60Preserved strength / non-significant paresis
Score < 48ICU-acquired weakness / significant quadriparesis
4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects critical illness polyneuropathy and myopathy characterized by axonal degeneration and muscle myosin loss.

5. Medical Background & Evidence
