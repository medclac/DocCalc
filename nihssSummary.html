NIH Stroke Scale (NIHSS)

Calculation ID: nihssSummary
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 11 neurological domain evaluations: Range 0–42
Normal Range / Target: NIHSS 0 = No stroke symptoms

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates 11 neurological domains: LOC, gaze, visual fields, facial palsy, motor arm/leg, ataxia, sensory, language, dysarthria, extinction.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Quantifies stroke severity, predicts large vessel occlusion (LVO), and guides intravenous thrombolysis and mechanical thrombectomy eligibility.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Ischemic Stroke
  • Transient Ischemic Attack (TIA)
  • Intracranial Hemorrhage

Key Medical Decision: Determines eligibility for IV Alteplase/Tenecteplase (<4.5h window) and emergent CT angiography for endovascular thrombectomy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Focal cerebral ischemia from thromboembolic arterial occlusion results in localized neurological deficits corresponding to the affected vascular territory.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Validated by Goldstein, Bertels, Davis (Stroke, 1989).
