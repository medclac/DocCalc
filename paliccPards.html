PALICC Pediatric ARDS (OI & OSI)

Calculation ID: paliccPards
1. Formula & Parameters
OI = (Paw × FiO2 × 100) ÷ PaO2 | OSI = (Paw × FiO2 × 100) ÷ SpO2
Normal Range / Target: OI < 4 | OSI < 5 (No PARDS)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Oxygenation Index (OI): Uses arterial blood gas PaO2, mean airway pressure (Paw), and FiO2.
  • Oxygen Saturation Index (OSI): Uses pulse oximetry SpO2 (≤97%) when arterial line is absent.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Essential for classifying Pediatric ARDS into Mild, Moderate, and Severe categories, guiding lung-protective ventilation and advanced rescue therapies.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pediatric ARDS (PARDS)
  • Sepsis-induced Lung Injury
  • Viral Pneumonia / Aspiration

Key Medical Decision: Determines PEEP titration, neuromuscular blockade, prone positioning, or cannulation for venovenous ECMO.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Alveolocapillary membrane disruption leads to protein-rich pulmonary edema, surfactant inactivation, intrapulmonary shunting, severe ventilation-perfusion mismatch, and decreased lung compliance.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Established by the Pediatric Acute Lung Injury Consensus Conference (PALICC Group, Pediatr Crit Care Med, 2015).
