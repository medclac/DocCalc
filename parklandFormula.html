Parkland Formula for Burn Resuscitation

Calculation ID: parklandFormula
1. Formula & Parameters
Total 24h Fluid = 4 mL × Weight (kg) × %TBSA (2nd & 3rd degree)
Normal Range / Target: Normative baseline hydration

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • 4 mL Lactated Ringer's per kg per % TBSA.
  • First 50% given over first 8 hours from injury.
  • Remaining 50% given over subsequent 16 hours.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Prevents burn shock and hypovolemia while avoiding fluid overload and compartment syndrome.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Major Thermal Burns
  • Burn Shock

Key Medical Decision: Directs initial IV fluid resuscitation rates, titrating hourly to urine output (0.5 mL/kg/h).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Thermal injury triggers systemic capillary leak, loss of intravascular oncotic pressure, massive third-space fluid shifts, and hypovolemic burn shock.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Pioneered by Baxter and the Parkland Hospital burn team (Clin Plast Surg, 1974).
