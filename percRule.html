PERC Rule for Pulmonary Embolism

Calculation ID: percRule
1. Formula & Parameters
All 8 criteria negative (Age<50, HR<100, SpO2≥95%, No hemoptysis, No estrogen, No prior VTE, No leg swelling, No recent surgery)
Normal Range / Target: All 8 negative = PE prevalence <1.4%

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Age < 50 years AND HR < 100 bpm.
  • SpO2 ≥ 95% on room air.
  • No unilateral leg swelling, hemoptysis, prior VTE, recent surgery, or estrogen use.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Eliminates blood draws and imaging in truly low-risk patients where disease prevalence outweighs testing harm.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Low-Risk Suspected Pulmonary Embolism

Key Medical Decision: Safely stops diagnostic workup for PE without ordering D-dimer or CTPA.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Evaluates clinical variables that together maintain post-test probability of PE well below the accepted test threshold.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Multicenter derivation and validation by Kline, Mitchell, Kabrhel et al. (J Thromb Haemost, 2004).
