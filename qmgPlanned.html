QMG (Quantitative Myasthenia Gravis)

Calculation ID: qmgPlanned
1. Formula & Parameters
Physician-administered objective endurance and weakness testing score (0–39)
Normal Range / Target: 0 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates ocular, facial, bulbar, respiratory, and limb muscle endurance.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Quantitative Myasthenia Gravis (QMG) score is a validated, physician-administered rating scale that measures objective muscle weakness and fatigability. Unlike patient-reported scales, QMG uses timed tests and dynamometry to grade neuromuscular junction reserve, serving as a primary endpoint in clinical trials.

Measures progressive failure of neuromuscular transmission during repetitive muscle contraction.
