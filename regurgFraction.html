Regurgitant Fraction Calculator

Calculation ID: regurgFraction
1. Formula & Parameters
RF = (Regurgitant Volume ÷ Total Forward Stroke Volume) × 100%
Normal Range / Target: < 30%

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Severe regurgitation defined as RF ≥ 50%.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Percentage of ventricular stroke volume regurgitating backward through an incompetent valve.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Mitral Regurgitation
  • Aortic Regurgitation

Key Medical Decision: Quantifies volumetric severity to guide surgical intervention.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Incompetent valve closure results in retrograde blood flow during systole or diastole, volume-loading the proximal chamber.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

ASE Guidelines (Zoghbi et al., J Am Soc Echocardiogr, 2017).
