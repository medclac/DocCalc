ROX Index for HFNC Success vs Failure

Calculation ID: roxIndex
1. Formula & Parameters
ROX = (SpO2 ÷ FiO2) ÷ Respiratory Rate
Normal Range / Target: ROX ≥ 4.88 at 12 hours indicates high probability of HFNC success

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • SpO2: Oxygen saturation percentage (%).
  • FiO2: Fraction of inspired oxygen (decimal).
  • Respiratory Rate: Breaths per minute.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Accurately identifies patients failing HFNC who require prompt endotracheal intubation, avoiding the harms of delayed intubation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Hypoxemic Respiratory Failure
  • Viral Pneumonia (e.g., COVID-19)
  • Acute Pulmonary Edema

Key Medical Decision: Decides whether to continue HFNC therapy or proceed immediately with rapid sequence intubation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects the balance between oxygenation efficiency and work of breathing; worsening tachypnea and FiO2 requirements rapidly depress the ROX score.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Validated in multicenter studies by Roca et al. (J Crit Care, 2016).
