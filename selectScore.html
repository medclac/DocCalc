SeLECT Score (Post-Ischemic Stroke Epilepsy)

Calculation ID: selectScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Scores Severity (NIHSS), Large artery etiology, Early seizures, Cortical involvement, Territory
Normal Range / Target: 0 – 1 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Predicts 1-year and 5-year risk of late unprovoked seizures following ischemic stroke.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The SeLECT score is a validated clinical instrument estimating the probability of post-stroke epilepsy following ischemic infarction. By evaluating stroke severity, cortical involvement, and early seizures, it helps clinicians assess long-term epileptogenic risk without routinely ordering prophylactic antiepileptic drugs.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Post-Ischemic Stroke Epilepsy Risk Assessment
