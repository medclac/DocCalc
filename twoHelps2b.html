2HELPS2B Score (cEEG Seizure Risk)

Calculation ID: twoHelps2b
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of cEEG patterns (Frequency, Periodic discharges, LPDs, Plus features, Spikes, BIRDs)
Normal Range / Target: 0 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores rhythmic frequency >2Hz, periodic discharges, LPDs, plus features, sporadic spikes, and BIRDs.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2HELPS2B score calculates the probability of seizures on continuous EEG (cEEG) in critically ill patients. It guides neuro-ICU monitoring duration, ensuring that patients with high-risk cEEG patterns undergo extended monitoring while sparing low-risk patients unnecessary resource utilization.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Critical Care Continuous EEG Monitoring
  • Non-Convulsive Status Epilepticus Detection

Key Medical Decision: Determines optimal duration of cEEG monitoring (1 hour vs ≥24–48 hours).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects critical cortical network hyperexcitability and electrographic seizure patterns.
