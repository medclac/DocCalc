Wells' Criteria for Pulmonary Embolism

Calculation ID: wellsPe
1. Formula & Parameters
Score = DVT signs (3) + PE #1 diagnosis (3) + Tachycardia (1.5) + Immobility/Surgery (1.5) + Prior VTE (1.5) + Hemoptysis (1) + Cancer (1)
Normal Range / Target: Score ≤ 4.0 = PE Unlikely

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Clinical DVT signs (+3.0) & PE #1 diagnosis (+3.0).
  • HR >100 (+1.5), Immobilization/Surgery (+1.5), Prior VTE (+1.5).
  • Hemoptysis (+1.0), Active malignancy (+1.0).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Prevents unnecessary radiation and contrast exposure by rationally directing diagnostic testing in suspected pulmonary embolism.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pulmonary Embolism (PE)
  • Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT)

Key Medical Decision: Decides whether to obtain a high-sensitivity D-dimer test or proceed directly to CT Pulmonary Angiography (CTPA).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Thrombus embolizes from deep venous system into pulmonary arterial tree, causing acute right ventricular strain, ventilation-perfusion mismatch, and hypoxemia.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Validated by Wells, Anderson, Rodger et al. (Ann Intern Med, 2001).
