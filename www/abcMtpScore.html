ABC Score for Massive Transfusion

Calculation ID: abcMtpScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Score = Penetrating (1) + SBP ≤90 (1) + HR ≥120 (1) + Positive FAST (1)
Normal Range / Target: Score 0–1 = MTP not indicated

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Penetrating trauma mechanism (+1).
  • Systolic blood pressure ≤ 90 mmHg (+1).
  • Heart rate ≥ 120 beats/min (+1).
  • Positive FAST ultrasound (+1).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Identifies exsanguinating hemorrhage early, prompting 1:1:1 balanced blood product resuscitation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Hemorrhagic Shock
  • Severe Polytrauma
  • Penetrating / Blunt Trauma

Key Medical Decision: Triggers immediate activation of Massive Transfusion Protocol and administration of Tranexamic Acid (TXA).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Severe acute hemorrhage depletes intravascular volume, triggering sympathetic reflex tachycardia, hypotension, and the lethal triad of trauma (coagulopathy, acidosis, hypothermia).

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Validated by Nunez, Voskresensky, Dossett et al. (J Trauma, 2009).
