Ankle–Brachial Index (ABI) in Extremity Trauma

Calculation ID: abiCalc
1. Formula & Parameters
ABI = Injured Ankle Systolic Pressure (DP or PT) ÷ Highest Brachial Systolic Pressure
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Ankle SBP: Highest Doppler systolic pressure in DP or PT.
  • Brachial SBP: Highest arm brachial pressure.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Ankle-Brachial Index (ABI) is an essential non-invasive bedside screening tool for occult arterial injury in severe extremity trauma and knee dislocations. An ABI below 0.90 exhibits high sensitivity for major vascular disruption, prompting urgent CT angiography and vascular surgical intervention.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Occult Vascular Injury Screening in Trauma
  • Knee Dislocation Vascular Assessment

Key Medical Decision: Triggers urgent CT angiography (CTA) and vascular surgery consultation when abnormal (<0.90).
