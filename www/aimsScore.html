AIMS (Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale)

Calculation ID: aimsScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 12 rating items across Facial/Oral, Extremity, and Trunk domains (0 to 28)
Normal Range / Target: 0 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Monitors drug-induced tardive dyskinesia in patients on dopamine-blocking medications.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Abnormal Involuntary Movement Scale (AIMS) is the standard psychiatric and neurological instrument for detecting and quantifying tardive dyskinesia induced by dopamine receptor antagonists (antipsychotics). Regular monitoring prevents irreversible extrapyramidal hyperkinetic movement disorders.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Tardive Dyskinesia Surveillance
  • Antipsychotic Medication Monitoring

Key Medical Decision: Prompts antipsychotic dose reduction, switching to atypical agents with low EPS risk, or initiating VMAT2 inhibitors.

AIMS ScoreInterpretation
0 – 1 PointsMinimal or subclinical movements
≥ 2 PointsPositive for abnormal involuntary movements (Tardive Dyskinesia)
4. Pathophysiological Background

Dopamine receptor supersensitivity within the basal ganglia striatal pathways.
