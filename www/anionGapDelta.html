Serum Anion Gap & Delta-Delta Gap

Calculation ID: anionGapDelta
1. Formula & Parameters
AG = Na − (Cl + HCO3) | Corrected AG = Measured AG + 2.5×(4.0 − Albumin) | Delta Ratio = ΔAG ÷ ΔHCO3
Normal Range / Target: Normal Anion Gap: 10–12 mEq/L

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Uncorrected Anion Gap = Sodium − (Chloride + Bicarbonate).
  • Albumin Correction: Add 2.5 for every 1 g/dL decrease in albumin below 4.0 g/dL.
  • Delta Ratio = Change in Anion Gap ÷ Change in Bicarbonate.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Essential diagnostic tool in metabolic acidosis, diabetic ketoacidosis, poisoning, and renal failure.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • High Anion Gap Metabolic Acidosis (HAGMA: MUDPILES)
  • Normal Anion Gap Metabolic Acidosis (NAGMA)
  • Mixed Acid-Base Disorders

Key Medical Decision: Directs toxicological screening, osmolar gap calculation, specific antidotes, and bicarbonate therapy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Accumulation of unmeasured anions (lactate, ketoacids, toxins) widens the anion gap; delta ratio reveals underlying concurrent acid-base disturbances.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Formulated in acid-base physiology and reviewed by Wrenn (Ann Emerg Med, 1990).
