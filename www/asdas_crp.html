ASDAS-CRP (Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity Score)

Calculation ID: asdas_crp
1. Formula & Parameters
0.121×BackPain + 0.058×Stiffness + 0.110×PatientGlobal + 0.073×Peripheral + 0.579×ln(CRP+1)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • BackPain, Stiffness, PatientGlobal, Peripheral: 0–10 scales.
  • CRP: mg/L.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity Score using CRP (ASDAS-CRP) is the preferred composite index for axial spondyloarthritis. By integrating patient-reported measures with an objective acute-phase reactant (CRP), ASDAS provides superior responsiveness and prognostic accuracy compared to BASDAI alone.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Axial Spondyloarthritis Treat-to-Target Monitoring

4. Pathophysiological Background

Combines patient-reported axial symptoms with hepatic acute-phase protein synthesis driven by IL-17 and TNF pathways.
