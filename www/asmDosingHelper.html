Antiseizure Medication (ASM) Renal Helper

Calculation ID: asmDosingHelper
1. Formula & Parameters
CrCl-based renal dose adjustment guidelines for major ASMs
Normal Range / Target: Normal renal clearance

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Calculates dosing adjustments for Levetiracetam, Lacosamide, Lamotrigine, and Phenytoin.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Provides essential renal dose adjustments for renally cleared antiseizure medications (such as levetiracetam and lacosamide), preventing drug toxicity and adverse neurological side effects in patients with chronic kidney disease.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Epilepsy Management in Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)
  • Hemodialysis Drug Dosing

Key Medical Decision: Optimizes ASM dosing to avoid neurotoxicity while maintaining therapeutic plasma concentrations.

MedicationRenal Elimination ProfileDosing Principle
LevetiracetamRenally cleared (~66% unchanged)Dose reduction or interval extension required for CrCl <80 mL/min
LacosamideRenally excreted metabolitesReduce daily dose by 25% when CrCl ≤30 mL/min
PhenytoinHepatic CYP metabolismNo direct renal adjustment, but monitor free levels in hypoalbuminemia/CKD
4. Pathophysiological Background

Accounts for diminished glomerular filtration rate and reduced renal clearance of parent drugs and active metabolites.
