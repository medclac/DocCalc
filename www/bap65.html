BAP-65 Score for Acute COPD Exacerbations

Calculation ID: bap65
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of BUN, Altered mental status, Pulse, and Age ≥65
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores BUN >25 mg/dL, altered mental status, pulse ≥109 bpm, and age ≥65.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The BAP-65 score predicts in-hospital mortality and risk of mechanical ventilation in patients hospitalized with acute COPD exacerbations. Stratifying patients into classes I through V guides appropriate ward versus intensive care unit admission.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute COPD Exacerbation Inpatient Triage

Key Medical Decision: Guides level of inpatient care (general ward vs ICU) and predicts mechanical ventilation risk.

BAP-65 ClassScoreIn-Hospital MortalityRecommended Site of Care
Class I0 points< 1.0%Outpatient management or short observation if stable
Class II1 point~ 1.0%General medical ward admission; monitored oral/IV bronchodilators
Class III2 points~ 5.6%Inpatient hospital admission; close monitoring of arterial blood gases
Class IV3 points~ 13.0%High-dependency unit or intensive care evaluation
Class V4 points~ 25.0%ICU ADMISSION: High risk of mechanical ventilation / NIV failure
4. Pathophysiological Background

Combines renal stress, neurological depression, tachycardia, and advanced age to assess exacerbation severity.

5. Medical Background & Evidence
