BASDAI (Bath Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity Index)

Calculation ID: basdai
1. Formula & Parameters
[Q1 + Q2 + Q3 + Q4 + ((Q5 + Q6) / 2)] ÷ 5
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Q1–Q4: Fatigue, back pain, peripheral pain, enthesitis (0–10).
  • Q5–Q6: Morning stiffness severity and duration (0–10).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Bath Ankylosing Spondylitis Disease Activity Index (BASDAI) is the gold-standard patient-reported outcome measure for quantifying axial disease activity in ankylosing spondylitis. A score of 4.0 or higher indicates active disease and serves as the standard threshold for initiating biologic therapies such as TNF or IL-17 inhibitors.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Axial Spondyloarthritis Disease Activity Monitoring
  • Biologic Therapy Eligibility Assessment

Key Medical Decision: Determines eligibility for TNF-alpha or IL-17 inhibitor biologic therapy when score is ≥4.0.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Quantifies subjective patient burden of spinal inflammation, enthesitis, fatigue, and stiffness.
