BISAP Score for Acute Pancreatitis

Calculation ID: bisapScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 5 clinical and laboratory parameters evaluated within 24 hours of admission
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • B: BUN >25 mg/dL. I: Impaired mental status. S: SIRS. A: Age >60. P: Pleural effusion.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The BISAP score is a rapid, simplified prognostic scoring system calculated within the first 24 hours of hospital presentation for acute pancreatitis. It accurately predicts in-hospital mortality and risk of severe organ failure, guiding early intensive care triage.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Early Acute Pancreatitis Triage

Key Medical Decision: Identifies high-risk patients within 24 hours who require immediate ICU admission and aggressive fluid management.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects early hemoconcentration, systemic inflammation, and third-space fluid accumulation.
