Bishop Score for Cervical Ripening

Calculation ID: bishopScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 5 cervical parameters (Dilatation, Effacement, Station, Consistency, Position; 0–13)
Normal Range / Target: Score ≥ 8 (Favourable)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Dilatation, Effacement, Station, Consistency, Position: Scored 0 to 3 or 0 to 2 points each.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Bishop score is the standard clinical scoring system used to assess pre-induction cervical readiness and predict the success of vaginal delivery. By grading five anatomical parameters (cervical dilation, effacement, fetal station, consistency, and position), clinicians can objectively determine whether a cervix is 'ripe' and ready for amniotomy or oxytocin, or whether pre-induction cervical ripening (prostaglandins or mechanical balloon catheters) is necessary.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pre-Induction Cervical Assessment
  • Labour & Delivery Suite Triage

Key Medical Decision: Determines whether direct labor induction (amniotomy/oxytocin) or cervical ripening (prostaglandins/balloon) is indicated.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects biochemical and structural remodeling of cervical extracellular matrix collagen and hyaluronic acid under estrogen and relaxin influence.
