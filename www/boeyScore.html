Boey Score for Perforated Peptic Ulcer

Calculation ID: boeyScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of 3 risk factors: Concomitant illness, Preop shock, Perforation duration >24h
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores severe medical illness, admission shock (SBP <90), and symptom duration >24 hours.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Boey score is a validated clinical risk stratification tool predicting postoperative mortality and morbidity in patients presenting with perforated peptic ulcer disease, guiding emergent surgical urgency and resuscitation intensity.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Perforated Peptic Ulcer Emergency Surgery

Key Medical Decision: Identifies high-risk perforation patients requiring aggressive ICU resuscitation and emergency surgical repair.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Quantifies physiological exhaustion, septic shock, and peritoneal contamination duration from perforated gastroduodenal ulcers.
