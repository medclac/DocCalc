CAM-ICU Delirium Screening

Calculation ID: camIcu
1. Formula & Parameters
Delirium Positive = Feature 1 (Acute change) AND Feature 2 (Inattention) AND (Feature 3 [Altered LOC] OR Feature 4 [Disorganized thinking])
Normal Range / Target: Negative (No delirium)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Feature 1: Acute onset or fluctuating mental status.
  • Feature 2: Inattention (SAVEHAART test).
  • Feature 3: Altered level of consciousness (RASS ≠ 0).
  • Feature 4: Disorganized thinking.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Detects hypoactive and hyperactive ICU delirium, which is independently associated with increased mortality and long-term cognitive impairment.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • ICU Delirium
  • Sepsis-Associated Encephalopathy
  • Medication Withdrawal

Key Medical Decision: Promotes non-pharmacological delirium bundles, sleep hygiene, early mobilization, and removal of deliriogenic medications.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Complex neuroinflammatory signaling, neurotransmitter imbalance (cholinergic deficit, dopamine excess), and cerebral hypoperfusion disrupt higher cortical function.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Validated by Ely, Inouye, Bernard et al. (Crit Care Med, 2001).
