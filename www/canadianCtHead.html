Canadian CT Head Rule (CCHR)

Calculation ID: canadianCtHead
1. Formula & Parameters
High-risk (GCS<15, skull fracture, age≥65, vomiting) or Medium-risk (amnesia>30m, dangerous mechanism)
Normal Range / Target: Low-risk = 100% sensitivity for neurosurgical lesion (No CT needed)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • High risk: GCS < 15 at 2h, skull fracture signs, ≥2 vomiting episodes, age ≥65.
  • Medium risk: Retrograde amnesia >30 min, dangerous mechanism.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Achieves 100% sensitivity for identifying patients requiring neurosurgical intervention while safely avoiding unnecessary CT scans.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Minor Blunt Head Trauma
  • Concussion
  • Mild TBI

Key Medical Decision: Determines necessity of emergent non-contrast head CT versus safe discharge home.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Blunt cranial impact generates acceleration-deceleration forces risking intracranial hemorrhage, skull fracture, and contusion.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Developed by Stiell, Wells, Vandemheen et al. (Lancet, 2001).
