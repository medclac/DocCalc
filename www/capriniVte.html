Caprini Score for Venous Thromboembolism (VTE) Risk

Calculation ID: capriniVte
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive multivariable scoring of VTE risk factors in surgical patients
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores age brackets, surgical duration/type, malignancy, prior VTE, and major orthopedic/spinal trauma.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Caprini score is a comprehensive risk assessment model for postoperative venous thromboembolism (DVT/PE). It provides granular risk stratification across surgical specialties, guiding tailored mechanical and pharmacological thromboprophylaxis regimens.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Postoperative VTE Risk Assessment & Prevention

Key Medical Decision: Prescribes risk-adapted venous thromboembolism prophylaxis (early ambulation vs IPC vs LMWH).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Integrates patient-specific, procedure-specific, and mobility-related risk factors for venous thrombosis.
