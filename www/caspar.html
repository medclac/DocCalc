CASPAR Criteria for Psoriatic Arthritis

Calculation ID: caspar
1. Formula & Parameters
Mandatory inflammatory arthritis entry + ≥3 points from Psoriasis, Nails, RF, Dactylitis, Bone formation
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Psoriasis: 1–2 pts. Nails: 1 pt. Negative RF: 1 pt. Dactylitis: 1 pt. Radiographic bone formation: 1 pt.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The CASPAR (Classification of Psoriatic Arthritis) criteria provide an exceptional diagnostic framework with 91% sensitivity and 99% specificity for psoriatic arthritis among patients with inflammatory joint, spine, or entheseal disease.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

4. Pathophysiological Background

Captures characteristic musculoskeletal manifestations associated with cutaneous and nail psoriasis.
