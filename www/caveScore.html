CAVE Score (Post-ICH Seizures)

Calculation ID: caveScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring: Cortical involvement + Age <65 + Volume >10mL + Early seizure
Normal Range / Target: 0 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores cortical involvement, age <65, ICH volume >10 mL, and early seizure within 7 days.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The CAVE score predicts the 2-year risk of late unprovoked seizures following spontaneous intracerebral hemorrhage (ICH). It helps clinicians identify patients who have developed epileptogenic cortical disruption and structural scarring, guiding discussions around post-stroke epilepsy surveillance.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Post-Intracerebral Hemorrhage Epilepsy

Key Medical Decision: Guides monitoring for secondary epilepsy post-ICH.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Hemosiderin deposition and glial scarring surrounding cortical hematomas generate hyperexcitable epileptic foci.
