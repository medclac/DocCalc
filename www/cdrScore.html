Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR)

Calculation ID: cdrScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Semi-structured global staging across 6 cognitive/functional domains (0 to 3)
Normal Range / Target: CDR 0

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates memory, orientation, judgment, community affairs, home hobbies, and personal care.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR) scale is a comprehensive semi-structured clinical interview staging the severity of dementia (specifically Alzheimer's disease). By rating cognitive and functional performance across six distinct domains, CDR provides a robust staging framework for clinical trials and longitudinal specialty care.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Alzheimer's Disease & Dementia Staging

Key Medical Decision: Stages dementia severity and tracks longitudinal cognitive decline in specialized memory clinics.

Measures progressive neocortical and hippocampal synaptic degeneration and functional loss.
