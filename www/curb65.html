CURB-65 Pneumonia Severity Score

Calculation ID: curb65
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 5 criteria: Confusion, Urea >7 mmol/L, RR ≥30, BP criteria, Age ≥65
Normal Range / Target: Score 0 – 1 (Low Risk)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Confusion: AMTS ≤8 or new disorientation.
  • Urea: >7.0 mmol/L (BUN >19 mg/dL).
  • RR: ≥30 breaths/min.
  • BP: SBP <90 or DBP ≤60 mmHg.
  • Age: ≥65 years.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The CURB-65 score is a widely validated clinical prediction rule used to estimate 30-day mortality in patients with community-acquired pneumonia (CAP). By stratifying risk based on mental status, renal function, respiratory rate, blood pressure, and age, it helps physicians determine the appropriate site of care—ranging from outpatient oral therapy to emergency hospital admission or intensive care unit escalation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CAP) Triage
  • Lower Respiratory Tract Infection Severity Staging

Key Medical Decision: Guides site of care decisions (outpatient vs inpatient ward vs ICU admission).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects acute systemic inflammation, hypoperfusion, end-organ stress, and physiological reserve depletion during infection.
