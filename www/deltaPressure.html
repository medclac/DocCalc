Compartment Perfusion Gradient (ΔP)

Calculation ID: deltaPressure
1. Formula & Parameters
ΔP = Diastolic Blood Pressure (DBP) − Measured Compartment Pressure (CP)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • DBP: Diastolic blood pressure (mmHg).
  • CP: Intracompartmental pressure (mmHg).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The compartment perfusion gradient (Delta Pressure, ΔP) is a crucial physiological measurement in acute extremity trauma. A gradient of 30 mmHg or less between diastolic blood pressure and tissue pressure signifies critical microvascular collapse and impending muscle necrosis, mandating emergent surgical fasciotomy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Compartment Syndrome (Tibial Shaft Fractures, Crush Injury)

Key Medical Decision: Triggers emergent surgical fasciotomy when tissue perfusion pressure falls below critical thresholds.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Elevated interstitial pressure within a non-compliant fascial compartment collapses capillaries, causing cellular hypoxia and myonecrosis.
