Driving Pressure & Static Compliance

Calculation ID: drivingPressureCalc
1. Formula & Parameters
ΔP = Pplat − PEEP | Cstat = Tidal Volume ÷ (Pplat − PEEP)
Normal Range / Target: Driving Pressure (ΔP) < 15 cmH2O | Cstat: 50–80 mL/cmH2O

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Plateau Pressure (Pplat): Measured during end-inspiratory hold (cmH2O).
  • Total PEEP: Positive end-expiratory pressure (cmH2O).
  • Tidal Volume: Vt in mL.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Driving pressure is the strongest ventilator-derived predictor of survival in ARDS; keeping ΔP < 15 cmH2O minimizes ventilator-induced lung injury (VILI).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • ARDS
  • Severe Asthma / COPD
  • Mechanical Ventilation

Key Medical Decision: Directs reduction of tidal volume (down to 4–6 mL/kg PBW), PEEP titration, and paralytic infusion.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Normalizes tidal volume to functional lung size (compliance); excessive driving pressure causes overdistention of aerated alveoli and volutrauma.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Landmark analysis by Amato, Meade, Slutsky et al. (N Engl J Med, 2015).
