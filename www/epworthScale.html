Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS)

Calculation ID: epworthScale
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 8 daily scenario dozing probability scores (0 to 24)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Rates chance of dozing in 8 daytime situations from 0 (never) to 3 (high chance).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Epworth Sleepiness Scale (ESS) is a standardized self-administered questionnaire measuring excessive daytime sleepiness (EDS). An ESS score >10 indicates pathological somnolence, prompting evaluation for sleep disorders such as obstructive sleep apnea, narcolepsy, or chronic sleep deprivation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Daytime Somnolence Evaluation
  • Sleep Disorders Screening

Key Medical Decision: Quantifies subjective daytime sleepiness and guides sleep clinic referral.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures central nervous system propensity to fall asleep during monotonous daily activities.
