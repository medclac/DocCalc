Fetal Doppler Flow Surveillance (UA, MCA, DV)

Calculation ID: fetalDopplerSurveillance
1. Formula & Parameters
Waveform analysis of Umbilical Artery, Middle Cerebral Artery, and Ductus Venosus
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates vascular resistance and fetal redistribution across Umbilical Artery, MCA, and Ductus Venosus.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Fetal Doppler ultrasonography is the cornerstone of surveillance for fetal growth restriction (FGR) and placental insufficiency. Serial evaluation of umbilical artery pulsatility index, middle cerebral artery brain sparing, and ductus venosus venous waveforms allows clinicians to stage fetal compromise and time delivery before irreversible stillbirth or anoxic brain injury occurs.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Early-Onset and Late-Onset Fetal Growth Restriction
  • Twin-Twin Transfusion Syndrome (TTTS)

Key Medical Decision: Determines optimal delivery timing in compromised growth-restricted fetuses to balance prematurity against stillbirth risk.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Progressive placental microvascular obliteration increases umbilical vascular resistance, triggering fetal cerebral vasodilation (brain sparing) and ultimately venous heart failure.
