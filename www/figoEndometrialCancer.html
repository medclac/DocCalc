1. Formula & Parameters
Pathological and molecular staging incorporating myometrial depth, LVSI, and molecular profile

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Integrates anatomical myometrial invasion, lymph node spread, and molecular classification (POLEmut, MMRd, NSMP, p53abn).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The revised 2023 FIGO staging system for endometrial carcinoma represents a major paradigm shift by integrating molecular classification (POLE mutations, mismatch repair deficiency, p53 abnormality) alongside traditional surgical pathology. This molecular integration ensures precision adjuvant therapy, preventing overtreatment in favorable molecular subgroups.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

4. Pathophysiological Background

Malignant transformation of endometrial glandular epithelium invading myometrium and lymphatic channels with distinct molecular drivers.
