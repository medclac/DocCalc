FIGO Leiomyoma (Fibroid) Subclassification

Calculation ID: figoLeiomyoma
1. Formula & Parameters
Anatomical subclassification system (Types 0 to 8) based on uterine wall location
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Classifies fibroids from purely intracavitary (Type 0) to completely subserosal (Type 6/7) or cervical (Type 8).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The FIGO leiomyoma subclassification system standardizes the anatomical description of uterine fibroids. Distinguishing submucosal fibroids (Types 0–2) that distort the endometrial cavity from intramural and subserosal lesions is critical for determining whether hysteroscopic, laparoscopic, or medical therapy is appropriate.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Uterine Fibroids / Leiomyomas
  • Abnormal Uterine Bleeding (AUB-L)

Key Medical Decision: Guides surgical route (hysteroscopic resection vs laparoscopic myomectomy vs hysterectomy).

FIGO TypeCategoryAnatomical LocationSurgical Approach
Types 0, 1, 2Submucosal (AUB-L_SM)Distorts endometrial cavity; major cause of heavy bleeding and infertilityHysteroscopic myomectomy (resectoscope)
Types 3, 4IntramuralConfined within myometrial wallLaparoscopic or open abdominal myomectomy, UAE, or medical therapy
Types 5, 6, 7SubserosalProtrudes outward onto peritoneal surface; causes bulk/pressure symptomsLaparoscopic myomectomy
Type 8Hybrid / ExtrauterineCervical, intraligamentary, or parasiticTargeted surgical excision or uterine artery embolization
4. Pathophysiological Background

Monoclonal benign smooth muscle tumors of the myometrium responding to estrogen and progesterone stimulation.
