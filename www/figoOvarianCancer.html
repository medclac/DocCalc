FIGO Ovarian & Fallopian Tube Cancer Staging

Calculation ID: figoOvarianCancer
1. Formula & Parameters
Surgical and pathological staging classification for epithelial ovarian and peritoneal cancer
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Staged surgically via peritoneal washings, hysterectomy, BSO, omentectomy, and lymphadenectomy.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The FIGO staging system for ovarian, fallopian tube, and primary peritoneal cancer standardizes surgical and pathological staging. Complete surgical cytoreduction combined with staging accuracy dictates adjuvant platinum-based chemotherapy and targeted maintenance regimens (PARP inhibitors, bevacizumab).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Ovarian, Fallopian Tube & Primary Peritoneal Carcinoma

Key Medical Decision: Establishes surgical stage to direct platinum-based chemotherapy and targeted maintenance therapy.

FIGO StageAnatomical DistributionStandard Primary Therapy
Stage I (IA, IB, IC)Confined to ovaries or fallopian tubesComplete surgical staging; adjuvant Carboplatin + Paclitaxel indicated for IC or high-grade serous
Stage II (IIA, IIB)Pelvic extension below pelvic brimPrimary debulking surgery followed by 6 cycles systemic IV chemotherapy
Stage III (IIIA, IIIB, IIIC)Peritoneal metastases beyond pelvis or retroperitoneal nodesMaximal cytoreductive surgery (aiming for R0 complete resection) + systemic Carboplatin/Paclitaxel ± Bevacizumab / PARP inhibitors
Stage IV (IVA, IVB)Pleural effusion or distant parenchymal organ metastasesNeoadjuvant chemotherapy (NACT) with interval debulking surgery (IDS) or palliative systemic chemotherapy
4. Pathophysiological Background

Malignant transformation of surface coelomic epithelium, fallopian tube secretory epithelium, or coelomic mesothelium.
