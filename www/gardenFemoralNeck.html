Garden Classification of Femoral Neck Fractures

Calculation ID: gardenFemoralNeck
1. Formula & Parameters
Radiographic classification (Stages I to IV) based on trabecular alignment
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Stages I–II: Non-displaced (impacted or complete).
  • Stages III–IV: Displaced (partial or complete dissociation).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Garden classification categorizes intracapsular femoral neck fractures by trabecular orientation on radiographs. Displaced fractures (Stages III and IV) carry a high risk of disrupting the precarious lateral epiphyseal blood supply, dictating the need for arthroplasty in elderly patients versus urgent reduction and fixation in young patients.

Useful Conditions / Indications:

Key Medical Decision: Guides surgical choice between internal fixation (cannulated screws) and arthroplasty (hemiarthroplasty / THA).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Shear and bending forces across the femoral neck disrupting the retinacular vessels supplying the femoral head.
