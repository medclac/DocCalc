2022 ACR/EULAR Giant Cell Arteritis (GCA) Criteria

Calculation ID: gca_criteria
1. Formula & Parameters
Mandatory age ≥50 entry + weighted criteria (headache, jaw claudication, visual loss, ESR/CRP, biopsy/US)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Entry: Age ≥50y. Criteria: Morning stiffness, visual loss, jaw claudication, headache, scalp tenderness, temporal artery abnormality, ESR/CRP elevation, biopsy/US positive, PET-CT.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2022 ACR/EULAR classification criteria for Giant Cell Arteritis provide a standardized diagnostic framework incorporating advanced imaging modalities (temporal artery ultrasound halo sign, PET-CT) alongside traditional clinical symptoms and biopsy findings.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Giant Cell Arteritis (Temporal Arteritis) Classification

Key Medical Decision: Classifies GCA (score ≥6 with age ≥50) to support high-dose glucocorticoid and biologic therapy decisions.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Granulomatous inflammation of large and medium-sized arteries dominated by T-cell and macrophage infiltration.
