Glasgow-Blatchford Bleeding Score (GBS)

Calculation ID: glasgowBlatchford
1. Formula & Parameters
Pre-endoscopic scoring incorporating BUN, Hemoglobin, SBP, Heart Rate, Melena, Syncope, Liver disease, and Heart failure
Normal Range / Target: Score 0–1 = Very low risk (<1% intervention risk)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN) and Hemoglobin thresholds.
  • Systolic blood pressure and heart rate.
  • Comorbidities: Melena, syncope, hepatic cirrhosis, heart failure.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Safely identifies patients eligible for outpatient management while prioritizing high-risk patients for urgent endoscopy and transfusion.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Upper Gastrointestinal Bleeding
  • Peptic Ulcer Disease
  • Variceal Bleeding

Key Medical Decision: Decides between outpatient discharge with elective endoscopy vs. emergency hospital admission and urgent upper endoscopy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Erosion of mucosal blood vessels by acid, peptic ulcers, or variceal hypertension leads to upper gastrointestinal hemorrhage, anemia, and uremia from blood protein digestion.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Developed by Blatchford, Murray, and Blatchford (Lancet, 2000).
