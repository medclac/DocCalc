Gustilo–Anderson Open Fracture Classification

Calculation ID: gustiloAnderson
1. Formula & Parameters
Wound size, soft-tissue injury, and contamination grading (Type I to IIIC)
Normal Range / Target: Closed Fracture (N/A)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Type I: <1 cm, clean.
  • Type II: 1–10 cm, moderate soft-tissue damage.
  • Type III: >10 cm, extensive soft-tissue damage, periosteal stripping, or vascular injury.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Gustilo-Anderson open fracture classification is the premier orthopedic trauma system used worldwide to categorize open fractures based on wound size, soft-tissue injury extent, and vascular compromise. This classification directly dictates initial empiric antibiotic protocols, surgical debridement urgency, and soft-tissue reconstruction planning to minimize devastating infection and nonunion rates.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Open Extremity Fractures
  • Orthopedic Trauma Triage & Debridement

Key Medical Decision: Determines empiric antibiotic coverage (cephalosporins vs aminoglycosides vs penicillin) and surgical fixation strategy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Quantifies high-energy kinetic transfer, periosteal stripping, and soft-tissue envelope destruction, which compromises local vascularity and bacterial clearance.
