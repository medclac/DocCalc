Hoehn and Yahr Staging

Calculation ID: hoehnYahr
1. Formula & Parameters
Clinical staging of Parkinson's disease progression (Stage 1 to 5)
Normal Range / Target: Stage 1

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Tracks bilateral spread, axial involvement, and postural reflex impairment.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Hoehn and Yahr scale is the standard clinical staging tool for Parkinson's disease progression. It categorizes the severity of parkinsonian signs—moving from unilateral involvement to severe bilateral postural instability and disability—guiding medical and surgical therapy (e.g., deep brain stimulation).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Parkinson's Disease Staging
  • Atypical Parkinsonian Disorders

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects progressive dopaminergic neuronal loss within the substantia nigra pars compacta and basal ganglia circuit degeneration.
