HOMA-IR (Insulin Resistance)

Calculation ID: homaIr
1. Formula & Parameters
HOMA-IR = (Fasting Glucose [mg/dL] × Fasting Insulin [µIU/mL]) ÷ 405
Normal Range / Target: < 1.9 (Normal Insulin Sensitivity)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Fasting Glucose: mg/dL.
  • Fasting Insulin: µIU/mL.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Homeostatic Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance (HOMA-IR) is a widely utilized mathematical surrogate reflecting hepatic and peripheral tissue resistance to insulin-mediated glucose uptake [cite: Matthews DR et al., 1985]. Elevated HOMA-IR values identify compensatory beta-cell hypersecretion maintaining euglycemia, signaling early metabolic syndrome, non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD), and heightened cardiovascular risk [cite: Matthews DR et al., 1985].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Metabolic Syndrome Screening [cite: Matthews DR et al., 2008]
  • Insulin Resistance in PCOS and NAFLD [cite: Matthews DR et al., 1985]

Key Medical Decision: Identifies early metabolic dysfunction prompting lifestyle modification and insulin-sensitizing interventions [cite: Matthews DR et al., 1985].

4. Pathophysiological Background

Models basal hepatic glucose output and peripheral insulin sensitivity under fasting steady-state conditions [cite: Matthews DR et al., 1985].
