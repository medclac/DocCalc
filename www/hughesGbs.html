Hughes GBS Disability Scale

Calculation ID: hughesGbs
1. Formula & Parameters
Functional mobility grading scale from Grade 0 (healthy) to Grade 6 (death)
Normal Range / Target: Grade 0

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades 0 to 6 based on ambulation independence and ventilation requirements.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Hughes Functional Disability Scale grades the clinical severity and mobility limitation of Guillain–Barré syndrome (GBS). A grade of 3 or higher indicates loss of independent ambulation, serving as the standard clinical threshold for initiating disease-modifying immunotherapy (IVIg or plasma exchange).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Guillain–Barré Syndrome (GBS)
  • Acute Inflammatory Demyelinating Polyneuropathy

Key Medical Decision: Indicates administration of intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIg) or plasma exchange (PLEX).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Immune-mediated demyelination or axonal damage of peripheral nerves leading to ascending flaccid paralysis.
