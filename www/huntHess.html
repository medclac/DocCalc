Hunt and Hess Scale for Aneurysmal SAH

Calculation ID: huntHess
1. Formula & Parameters
Clinical severity grading (1 to 5) adjusted for systemic disease
Normal Range / Target: Grade 1 (Asymptomatic / Mild)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades 1–5 based on headache, nuchal rigidity, focal deficit, and sensorium. Severe systemic disease adds +1.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Hunt and Hess scale stratifies clinical neurological condition and perioperative surgical risk in patients with aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage (SAH). Lower grades correlate with excellent surgical outcomes, whereas higher grades (IV–V) convey severe risk of cerebral edema, vasospasm, and mortality, dictating urgent aneurysm securing and intensive neurocritical care.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Aneurysmal Subarachnoid Hemorrhage
  • Cerebral Aneurysm Rupture

Key Medical Decision: Determines surgical urgency, timing of aneurysm clipping vs coiling, and intensity of cerebral vasospasm surveillance.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects initial bleeding severity, acute intracranial pressure elevation, and global cerebral ischemia from the aneurysmal rupture.
