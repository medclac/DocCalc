Hypoglycemia Classification

Calculation ID: hypoClassification
1. Formula & Parameters
ADA Level 1 (54–70), Level 2 (<54), Level 3 (Cognitive impairment)
Normal Range / Target: > 70 mg/dL

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Blood Glucose: mg/dL.
  • Assistance: Required or not.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Prevents fatal neuroglycopenia and cardiac arrhythmias [cite: International Hypoglycaemia Study Group, 2017].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Hypoglycemia Management [cite: International Hypoglycaemia Study Group, 2017]

Key Medical Decision: Triggers Rule of 15 treatment or parenteral rescue [cite: International Hypoglycaemia Study Group, 2017].
