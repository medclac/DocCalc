Kellgren–Lawrence Osteoarthritis Grading System

Calculation ID: kellgrenLawrence
1. Formula & Parameters
Radiographic grading scale (Grade 0 to 4) evaluating osteophytes and joint space narrowing
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades osteoarthritis severity from doubtful joint space narrowing (Grade 1) to severe bone deformity and large osteophytes (Grade 4).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Kellgren-Lawrence grading system is the universal standard for scoring radiographic knee and hip osteoarthritis. By quantifying osteophyte formation, subchondral sclerosis, and joint space narrowing, it guides non-operative management versus joint arthroplasty timing.
