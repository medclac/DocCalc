Lupus Low Disease Activity State (LLDAS)

Calculation ID: lldas
1. Formula & Parameters
Simultaneous fulfillment of 5 clinical criteria including SLEDAI ≤4 and prednisone ≤7.5 mg/day
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Criteria: SLEDAI ≤4, no major organ activity, no new features, PGA ≤1.0, prednisone ≤7.5 mg/day, stable maintenance therapy.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Lupus Low Disease Activity State (LLDAS) is a validated treat-to-target clinical endpoint for SLE. Achieving sustained LLDAS is strongly associated with decreased flare rates, halted irreversible organ damage accrual, and improved long-term survival.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • SLE Treat-to-Target Management

Key Medical Decision: Validates safe control of systemic lupus disease activity and guides glucocorticoid tapering.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Represents a suppressed immunological state where tissue inflammation and autoantibody-mediated damage are controlled.
