2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The McDonald Criteria are the definitive international diagnostic standards for multiple sclerosis. By incorporating advanced MRI criteria for dissemination in space and time—along with cerebrospinal fluid oligoclonal bands—they enable prompt, accurate diagnosis of MS, allowing early initiation of highly effective disease-modifying therapies.
