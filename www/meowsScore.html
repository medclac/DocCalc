2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Modified Early Obstetric Warning System (MEOWS) provides a systematic track-and-trigger vital sign protocol for inpatient obstetric wards. By flagging abnormal physiological trends early, MEOWS enables rapid multidisciplinary intervention for maternal sepsis, postpartum hemorrhage, and preeclampsia before catastrophic maternal collapse occurs.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Obstetric Inpatient Sepsis & Shock Surveillance
  • Postpartum Early Warning Monitoring

Key Medical Decision: Triggers urgent medical review by senior obstetric and anesthetic registrars within 15 minutes of a red alert.
