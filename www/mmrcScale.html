mMRC (Modified Medical Research Council) Dyspnea Scale

Calculation ID: mmrcScale
1. Formula & Parameters
5-point functional grading scale (Grade 0 to 4) evaluating breathlessness during activity
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades breathlessness from strenuous exercise (0) to breathlessness at rest/dressing (4).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The mMRC dyspnea scale is a simple, widely validated tool quantifying the functional disability caused by breathlessness in chronic respiratory diseases like COPD. An mMRC grade ≥2 defines high symptom burden in the GOLD classification scheme.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Chronic Respiratory Disease Symptom Burden Assessment

Key Medical Decision: Classifies COPD symptom severity (Group A/B vs E) and indicates need for pulmonary rehabilitation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures functional impact of dynamic hyperinflation and respiratory muscle work during exertion.
