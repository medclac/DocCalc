2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The National Emergency X-Radiography Utilization Study (NEXUS) criteria provide a validated clinical decision instrument to safely clear the cervical spine without radiography in blunt trauma patients who meet 5 specific low-risk conditions, avoiding unnecessary radiation and collar immobilization.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Blunt Trauma Cervical Spine Clearance

Key Medical Decision: Safely removes cervical collar immobilization without radiographic imaging in low-risk trauma patients.
