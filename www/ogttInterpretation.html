Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates fasting, 1-hour, and 2-hour venous plasma glucose following 75g oral anhydrous glucose.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Universal screening for gestational diabetes mellitus (GDM) using the 75g oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) identifies maternal hyperglycemia linked to fetal macrosomia, neonatal hypoglycemia, shoulder dystocia, and future maternal type 2 diabetes. Meeting or exceeding any single threshold confirms GDM.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Gestational Diabetes Screening (24–28 weeks gestation)

Key Medical Decision: Establishes GDM diagnosis and initiates medical nutrition therapy, blood glucose self-monitoring, and pharmacotherapy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Placental diabetogenic hormones (human placental lactogen, GH-V, progesterone) induce profound maternal peripheral insulin resistance.
