O-RADS Ultrasound Stratification (Adnexal Lesions)

Calculation ID: oradsClassification
1. Formula & Parameters
ACR ultrasound risk categorization (O-RADS 1 to 5) based on morphology and Doppler
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Categorizes adnexal mass malignancy risk based on cyst architecture, solid components, and vascularity.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The O-RADS (Ovarian-Adnexal Reporting and Data System) ultrasound risk stratification system provides standardized terminology and malignancy risk estimates for adnexal lesions. It optimizes the balance between ensuring prompt oncologic referral for high-risk masses and avoiding unnecessary surgery for benign cysts.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Adnexal Pelvic Mass Ultrasound Triage

Key Medical Decision: Determines follow-up interval, pelvic MRI requirement, or gynecologic oncology surgical referral.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Categorizes sonographic features of epithelial, stromal, and germ cell ovarian neoplasms.
