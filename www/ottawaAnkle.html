2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Ottawa Ankle Rules are highly sensitive (>98%) clinical decision guidelines designed to safely rule out malleolar and midfoot fractures without radiographs. Adhering to these rules significantly reduces emergency department waiting times, unnecessary radiation exposure, and healthcare costs.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Ankle and Midfoot Trauma Triage

Key Medical Decision: Safely avoids unnecessary foot and ankle radiographs in low-risk trauma patients.
