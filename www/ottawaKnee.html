Ottawa Knee Rule for Acute Knee Trauma

Calculation ID: ottawaKnee
1. Formula & Parameters
Clinical decision criteria: Age ≥55, patella/fibula tenderness, flex <90°, non-weightbearing
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates age, patellar tenderness, fibular head tenderness, knee flexion to 90°, and 4-step weight-bearing.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Ottawa Knee Rule provides an evidence-based clinical decision instrument with near 100% sensitivity for ruling out acute knee fractures. Utilizing specific physical exam triggers, it allows emergency clinicians to safely avoid routine knee X-rays in minor trauma.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Knee Injury Triage

Key Medical Decision: Safely excludes acute knee fractures without requiring radiographic imaging.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Detects structural bony disruptions of the patella, proximal tibia, and fibular head.
