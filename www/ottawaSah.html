Ottawa SAH Rule for Acute Headache

Calculation ID: ottawaSah
1. Formula & Parameters
6 clinical screening criteria for subarachnoid hemorrhage in alert patients
Normal Range / Target: Ottawa Rule Negative

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Age ≥40, neck pain/stiffness, witnessed LOC, exertion onset, thunderclap, limited neck flexion.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Ottawa SAH Rule is a highly sensitive clinical decision instrument designed to rule out subarachnoid hemorrhage in alert, cognitively intact adult patients presenting with acute non-traumatic headache that reached peak intensity within one hour. With a sensitivity near 100%, a negative rule safely excludes SAH without requiring CT head or lumbar puncture.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Thunderclap Headache Triage

Key Medical Decision: Safely avoids unnecessary neuroimaging in low-risk headache presentations while ensuring 100% sensitivity for SAH.
