FIGO PALM-COEIN Classification for AUB

Calculation ID: palmCoeinClassification
1. Formula & Parameters
Categorizes abnormal uterine bleeding into Structural (PALM) and Non-structural (COEIN) causes
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • PALM: Polyp, Adenomyosis, Leiomyoma, Malignancy. COEIN: Coagulopathy, Ovulatory, Endometrial, Iatrogenic, Not classified.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The FIGO PALM-COEIN system is the international diagnostic framework for investigating abnormal uterine bleeding (AUB) in reproductive-aged women. It systematically categorizes potential etiologies into structural uterine lesions (PALM) and non-structural functional disorders (COEIN), guiding targeted medical and surgical management.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Abnormal Uterne Bleeding (AUB) / Menorrhagia

Key Medical Decision: Directs diagnostic workup (ultrasound, hysteroscopy, endometrial biopsy, coagulation labs) and targeted medical/surgical therapy.

ComponentCategoryClassificationFirst-Line Workup & Therapy
PALMStructural CausesPolyp, Adenomyosis, Leiomyoma, MalignancyTargeted surgical therapy (polypectomy, myomectomy, hysterectomy, LNG-IUS) or biopsy
COEINNon-Structural CausesCoagulopathy, Ovulatory, Endometrial, Iatrogenic, Not classifiedMedical management: Tranexamic acid, oral progestins, combined OCPs, LNG-IUS, hematology consultation
4. Pathophysiological Background

Differentiates focal anatomical pathology from systemic hormonal, endocrine, or hemostatic dysregulation.
