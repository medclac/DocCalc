Parkland Formula & Revised Baux Score (Burns)

Calculation ID: parklandBauxBurns
1. Formula & Parameters
Parkland: 4 mL × Weight (kg) × % TBSA | Baux: Age + % TBSA + [17 if inhalation]
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Parkland: 24h Lactated Ringer's volume. Baux: 30-day mortality prediction index.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Parkland formula dictates emergency fluid resuscitation requirements in severe burn injuries, while the Revised Baux score provides an accurate prediction of burn mortality, guiding trauma triage and family counselling.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Thermal Burn Resuscitation & Trauma Triage

Key Medical Decision: Calculates 24-hour crystalloid infusion rates and predicts survival probability in major burns.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Severe cutaneous thermal injury induces massive capillary leak, systemic inflammatory response, and fluid sequestration.
