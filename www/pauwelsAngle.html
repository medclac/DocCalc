Pauwels Classification of Femoral Neck Fractures

Calculation ID: pauwelsAngle
1. Formula & Parameters
Angle of fracture line relative to the horizontal pelvic plane (<30°, 30°–50°, >50°)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Type I: <30°. Type II: 30°–50°. Type III: >50° (High vertical shear).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Pauwels classification evaluates the biomechanical shear angle of femoral neck fractures. Type III fractures (>50°) experience massive vertical shear stresses that predispose to mechanical failure, nonunion, and varus collapse, requiring specialized fixation or valgus osteotomy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Femoral Neck Biomechanical Assessment

Key Medical Decision: Guides selection of sliding hip screws, fixed-angle devices, or intertrochanteric valgus osteotomy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures the biomechanical vector angle transforming axial load into shear forces across the femoral neck.
