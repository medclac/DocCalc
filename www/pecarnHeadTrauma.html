PECARN Pediatric Head Trauma Rule

Calculation ID: pecarnHeadTrauma
1. Formula & Parameters
Risk Stratification Algorithm (<2 years and ≥2 years cohorts)
Normal Range / Target: GCS 15 with no high-risk or intermediate-risk features = Very Low Risk (<0.05%)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Age < 2 years: Evaluates GCS < 15, palpable skull fracture, non-frontal hematoma, LOC duration, severe mechanism, and parental behavior.
  • Age ≥ 2 years: Evaluates GCS < 15, basilar skull fracture signs, history of LOC, vomiting, severe mechanism, and severe headache.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Identifies children at very low risk of clinically important traumatic brain injury (ciTBI), safely avoiding unnecessary cranial CT radiation exposure in pediatric patients.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Blunt Head Trauma
  • Concussion
  • Scalp Hematoma
  • Suspected Skull Fracture

Key Medical Decision: Decides between immediate non-contrast head CT, 4–6 hour ED observation, or safe outpatient discharge without imaging.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Pediatric cranial anatomy features higher water content, thinner skulls, and developing subarachnoid spaces, making them vulnerable to intracranial hemorrhage and shearing injury under rotational deceleration forces.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Developed via a landmark prospective multicenter observational cohort study across 25 emergency departments by Kuppermann et al. (Lancet, 2009).
