Pediatric Airway & Equipment Sizing

Calculation ID: pediatricAirwaySizing
1. Formula & Parameters
Cuffed ETT = (Age/4) + 3.5 | Depth = (Age/2) + 12 | Shock = 2 J/kg then 4 J/kg
Normal Range / Target: Age-matched normative values

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Cuffed ETT Internal Diameter (mm) = (Age in years / 4) + 3.5.
  • Oral ETT Depth (cm at lip) = (Age in years / 2) + 12.
  • Defibrillation Energy: Initial 2 J/kg, subsequent 4 J/kg.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Prevents airway trauma, mainstem intubation, or hypoxia during emergency pediatric intubation and resuscitation.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Cardiac Arrest
  • Severe Status Asthmaticus
  • Traumatic Brain Injury
  • Acute Respiratory Failure

Key Medical Decision: Directs rapid sequence intubation (RSI) tube selection, depth marking, chest tube insertion, and PALS defibrillation energy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

The pediatric airway features a funnel-shaped larynx with the narrowest portion at the cricoid ring.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Standardized by the American Heart Association (AHA) PALS Guidelines 2020.
