Pheochromocytoma Biochemical Screening

Calculation ID: pheoScreenRef
1. Formula & Parameters
Plasma/Urinary Metanephrines >3–4× ULN
Normal Range / Target: Within normal limits

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Metanephrines: Plasma free or urinary fractionated.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Detects autonomous catecholamine production.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

    Pheochromocytoma / Paraganglioma

Key Medical Decision: Screens for chromaffin tumors.

Metanephrine LevelInterpretation
<2× ULNEquivocal / Borderline
>3–4× ULNHighly Suggestive
4. Pathophysiological Background

Intratumoral O-methylated catecholamines.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Lenders JW et al. JCEM. 2014.
