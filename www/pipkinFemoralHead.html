Pipkin Classification (Femoral Head Fractures)

Calculation ID: pipkinFemoralHead
1. Formula & Parameters
Anatomical classification (Types I to IV) of femoral head fractures with hip dislocation
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Type I: Infrafoveal. Type II: Suprafoveal. Type III: With femoral neck fracture. Type IV: With acetabular fracture.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Pipkin classification categorizes rare femoral head fracture-dislocations of the hip. Recognizing the fragment location relative to the fovea capitis and associated neck or acetabular fractures dictates urgent reduction and surgical reconstruction to preserve hip stability and prevent osteonecrosis.

4. Pathophysiological Background

High-energy axial loading of the femur with the hip flexed, causing the femoral head to shear against the posterior acetabular wall.
