Periprosthetic Joint Infection (PJI) ICM Score

Calculation ID: pjiCriteria
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of minor diagnostic criteria points (Serum markers, Synovial fluid WBC/PMN, Leukocyte esterase)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Major: Sinus tract or 2 positive cultures (Definitive PJI). Minor: CRP/D-dimer (2), ESR (1), Synovial WBC >3k (3), Synovial PMN >70% (2), Leukocyte esterase (3). Score ≥6 = Infected.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2018 International Consensus Meeting (ICM) criteria provide an objective, standardized scoring system to diagnose periprosthetic joint infection (PJI) in total hip and knee arthroplasties. Accurate diagnosis is vital to dictate surgical management (DAIR vs 1-stage vs 2-stage exchange).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Failed Total Joint Arthroplasty / Suspected PJI

Key Medical Decision: Establishes definitive PJI diagnosis (score ≥6) to guide surgical revision protocols.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures systemic and local synovial inflammatory and biochemical markers triggered by bacterial biofilm formation on prosthetic implants.
