Pelvic Organ Prolapse Quantification (POP-Q) System

Calculation ID: popqClassification
1. Formula & Parameters
Anatomical measurement of 6 vaginal points relative to the hymenal ring (0 cm)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Measures descent of anterior, apical, and posterior vaginal compartments relative to the hymen.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The POP-Q system is the objective, reproducible international standard for staging pelvic organ prolapse. By measuring anatomical points relative to the hymen during maximal Valsalva strain, urogynecologists can precisely stage cystoceles, uterine prolapse, enteroceles, and rectoceles to guide pessary fitting or reconstructive surgery.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pelvic Organ Prolapse (Cystocele, Rectocele, Uterine Descent)

Key Medical Decision: Determines stage-appropriate therapy ranging from pelvic floor physical therapy and pessaries to reconstructive surgery.

POP-Q StageAnatomical Criteria (Leading Edge Relative to Hymen)Clinical FeaturesManagement Options
Stage 0Leading edge < -3 cm (no descent)Normal anatomical pelvic supportRoutine pelvic floor muscle training (Kegels)
Stage 1Leading edge is > -3 cm but < -1 cmMild descent inside vaginaAsymptomatic: pelvic floor physical therapy
Stage 2Leading edge is between -1 cm and +1 cmDescent to the level of the introitus/hymenVaginal pessary fitting or conservative surveillance
Stage 3Leading edge is > +1 cm but < (tvl - 2 cm)Prolapse protrudes beyond hymenVaginal pessary vs reconstructive pelvic surgery (sacrocolpopexy, hysteropexy, colporrhaphy)
Stage 4 (Procidentia)Leading edge is ≥ (tvl - 2 cm)Complete eversion / procidentia of the vagina/uterusSurgical reconstruction with apical suspension or colpocleisis
4. Pathophysiological Background

Failure or weakening of endopelvic fascia, uterosacral-cardinal ligament complexes, and perineal body support.
