Postpartum Haemorrhage (PPH) Severity Protocol

Calculation ID: pphSeverityAssessment
1. Formula & Parameters
Cumulative blood loss volume staging combined with vital sign stability
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Stages PPH severity from Stage 1 (<1000 mL) to Stage 3 (≥1500 mL or hemodynamic instability).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Postpartum hemorrhage is a leading cause of preventable maternal mortality. Standardized PPH protocols ensure rapid recognition, quantitative blood loss measurement, early administration of uterotonics and tranexamic acid, and activation of massive transfusion protocols before irreversible hemorrhagic shock and coagulopathy occur.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Obstetric Haemorrhage
  • Uterine Atony / Surgical Trauma

Key Medical Decision: Triggers escalation from first-line uterotonics to second-line medical therapy, intrauterine balloon tamponade, and massive transfusion protocol.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Failure of physiological myometrial fiber contraction (atony) following placental delivery, leading to open maternal spiral arterioles.

5. Medical Background & Evidence
