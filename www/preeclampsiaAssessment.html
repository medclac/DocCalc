Preeclampsia Diagnostic & Severity Staging

Calculation ID: preeclampsiaAssessment
1. Formula & Parameters
Gestational BP thresholds combined with proteinuria and systemic end-organ features
Normal Range / Target: BP < 140/90 mmHg, No Proteinuria

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Evaluates blood pressure (≥140/90 or ≥160/110 mmHg), UPCR ≥0.3, and severe end-organ features.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Preeclampsia is a leading cause of maternal and perinatal morbidity and mortality worldwide. Accurate diagnosis and severity staging per ACOG 2020 guidelines are essential for timing delivery, initiating blood pressure control, and administering magnesium sulfate seizure prophylaxis to prevent maternal eclampsia, stroke, and multi-organ failure.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Gestational Hypertension & Preeclampsia
  • HELLP Syndrome & Eclampsia Prevention

Key Medical Decision: Determines need for inpatient admission, antihypertensive therapy, magnesium sulfate prophylaxis, and delivery timing.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Abnormal placental spiral artery remodeling leads to placental ischemia, systemic endothelial dysfunction, vasoconstriction, and capillary leak.
