QUICKI Insulin Sensitivity Index

Calculation ID: quickiIndex
1. Formula & Parameters
QUICKI = 1 ÷ [log(Fasting Glucose mg/dL) + log(Fasting Insulin µIU/mL)]
Normal Range / Target: ≥ 0.33 (Preserved Sensitivity)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Fasting Glucose: mg/dL.
  • Fasting Insulin: µIU/mL.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Quantitative Insulin Sensitivity Check Index (QUICKI) is a mathematical transformation providing superior correlation with gold-standard euglycemic clamp studies [cite: Katz A et al., 2000]. Values below 0.33 denote impaired peripheral insulin sensitivity [cite: Katz A et al., 2000].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Insulin Resistance Screening [cite: Katz A et al., 2000]

Key Medical Decision: Assesses cellular glucose disposal and metabolic flexibility [cite: Katz A et al., 2000].

4. Pathophysiological Background

Logarithmic transformation normalizes skewed biological distributions of insulin and glucose [cite: Katz A et al., 2000].
