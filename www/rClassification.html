Residual Tumor (R) Classification (UICC / AJCC)

Calculation ID: rClassification
1. Formula & Parameters
Pathological margin status classification: R0 (negative), R1 (microscopic positive), R2 (macroscopic residual)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • R0: Complete microscopic/macroscopic resection. R1: Microscopic positive margins. R2: Gross residual tumor.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Residual Tumor (R) classification is the universal pathological descriptor defining the completeness of cancer surgery. Achieving an R0 resection is the primary surgical objective for curative-intent oncologic procedures to prevent local recurrence.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

4. Pathophysiological Background

Denotes the presence or absence of residual neoplastic tissue remaining in the patient after surgery.
