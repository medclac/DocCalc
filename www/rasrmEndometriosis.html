Revised ASRM (rASRM) Endometriosis Staging

Calculation ID: rasrmEndometriosis
1. Formula & Parameters
Surgical scoring system quantifying peritoneal/ovarian implants and adhesions (Stage I to IV)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores superficial vs deep peritoneal implants, ovarian endometriomas, cul-de-sac obliteration, and adnexal adhesions.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The revised American Society for Reproductive Medicine (rASRM) classification is the standard surgical staging system for endometriosis. It quantifies the anatomical extent of peritoneal implants, endometriomas, and adhesions, guiding fertility preservation and surgical excision strategies.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pelvic Endometriosis Staging
  • Infertility & Pelvic Pain Surgery

Key Medical Decision: Guides surgical excision, medical suppression, and assisted reproductive technology (ART) referral.

Stage I (Minimal)1 – 5 PointsFew superficial implantsSurgical excision/ablation; favourable spontaneous fertility rates
Stage II (Mild)6 – 15 PointsMore numerous superficial implants in cul-de-sacLaparoscopic excision; medical suppression with dienogest/GnRH
Stage III (Moderate)16 – 40 PointsEndometriomas present and peritubal adhesionsCystectomy, adhesiolysis; referral for assisted reproductive technologies (IVF)
Stage IV (Severe)> 40 PointsLarge endometriomas, extensive dense adhesions, cul-de-sac obliterationMultidisciplinary laparoscopic excision (colorectal/urology involvement); high infertility burden
4. Pathophysiological Background

Retrograde menstruation and coelomic metaplasia leading to ectopic endometrial glands and stroma implanting within the peritoneal cavity.
