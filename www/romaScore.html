Risk of Ovarian Malignancy Algorithm (ROMA)

Calculation ID: romaScore
1. Formula & Parameters
Multi-marker biomarker algorithm combining HE4, CA-125, and menopausal status
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Calculates predictive index using serum HE4, CA-125, and menopausal status.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Risk of Ovarian Malignancy Algorithm (ROMA) integrates serum HE4 and CA-125 biomarker concentrations with menopausal status to estimate the probability of epithelial ovarian cancer in women presenting with a pelvic mass. It provides higher sensitivity and specificity than CA-125 alone.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Combines human epididymis protein 4 (HE4) and cancer antigen 125 (CA-125) overexpression by epithelial ovarian tumors.
