Rumack-Matthew Nomogram (Acetaminophen)

Calculation ID: rumackMatthew
1. Formula & Parameters
Treatment line starts at 150 mcg/mL at 4 hours post-ingestion, halving every 4 hours
Normal Range / Target: Below treatment line = Low hepatotoxicity risk

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Hours post single acute ingestion (4 to 24 hours).
  • Serum acetaminophen concentration in mcg/mL.
  • 150-line semi-logarithmic threshold.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Predicts severe hepatotoxicity and mandates timely administration of N-acetylcysteine (NAC) antidote therapy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Acetaminophen Overdose
  • Paracetamol Toxicity

Key Medical Decision: Determines immediate initiation of intravenous or oral N-acetylcysteine (NAC) therapy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Overdose saturates glucuronidation and sulfation pathways, shunting paracetamol into cytochrome P450 metabolism to produce toxic NAPQI metabolite, depleting glutathione and causing centrilobular hepatic necrosis.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Pioneered by Rumack and Matthew (Pediatrics, 1975).
