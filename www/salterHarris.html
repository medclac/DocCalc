Salter–Harris Physeal Fracture Classification

Calculation ID: salterHarris
1. Formula & Parameters
Pediatric growth plate classification (Types I to V) based on fracture plane
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Type I: Slipped. Type II: Above. Type III: Lower. Type IV: Through. Type V: Rammed (Crush).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Salter-Harris classification is the universal system for categorizing pediatric physeal (growth plate) fractures. Recognizing the specific fracture plane relative to the germinal and hypertrophic zones predicts the risk of premature growth arrest, angular deformity, and limb length discrepancy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

4. Pathophysiological Background

Shearing, tension, or axial compression forces traversing the cartilaginous growth plate in skeletally immature patients.
