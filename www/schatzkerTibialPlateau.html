Schatzker Tibial Plateau Classification

Calculation ID: schatzkerTibialPlateau
1. Formula & Parameters
Radiological classification (Types I to VI) of proximal tibial plateau fractures
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Types I–III: Lateral plateau. Type IV: Medial plateau. Types V–VI: Bicondylar and metaphyseal-diaphyseal dissociation.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Schatzker classification is the classic radiological system for categorizing tibial plateau fractures. It categorizes injury patterns from simple lateral wedge splits to complex bicondylar and dissociative injuries, guiding surgical approaches, bone grafting, and dual-plate osteosynthesis.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Axial loading combined with valgus or varus bending moments crushing the proximal tibial articular surface.
