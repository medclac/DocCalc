2019 EULAR/ACR Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) Criteria

Calculation ID: sle_criteria
1. Formula & Parameters
Mandatory positive ANA entry + weighted points across 7 clinical and 3 immunological domains
Normal Range / Target: < 10 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Entry: ANA ≥1:80.
  • Domains: Constitutional, hematologic, neuropsychiatric, mucocutaneous, serosal, musculoskeletal, renal, antiphospholipid, complement, and specific antibodies.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The 2019 EULAR/ACR classification criteria for Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE) incorporate rigorous weighted domain scoring with a mandatory ANA entry criterion. This modern framework provides exceptional sensitivity for early disease and high specificity for confirmed clinical trials and specialized clinical care.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Systemic Lupus Erythematosus Diagnosis & Classification

Key Medical Decision: Classifies definite SLE (score ≥10 with clinical criteria) to initiate hydroxychloroquine and immunosuppressive therapy.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Captures multisystem autoimmune tissue injury driven by loss of self-tolerance, autoantibody production, and immune complex deposition.
