SLEDAI-2K (Systemic Lupus Disease Activity Index 2000)

Calculation ID: sledai2k
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of 24 weighted clinical and laboratory variables present within the preceding 30 days
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores active lupus manifestations across organ systems over the past 30 days.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Systemic Lupus Disease Activity Index 2000 (SLEDAI-2K) is a validated continuous index quantifying active inflammatory disease in SLE. Tracking SLEDAI scores over time helps clinicians evaluate treatment response and detect acute lupus flares across vital organ domains.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Systemic Lupus Erythematosus Flare Monitoring

Key Medical Decision: Guides escalation or tapering of systemic corticosteroids and cytotoxic/biologic immunosuppression.

SLEDAI-2K ScoreActivity StateClinical Action
0 pointsInactive DiseaseMaintain baseline hydroxychloroquine therapy
1 – 5 pointsMild Disease ActivityMinor adjustment; monitor closely
6 – 10 pointsModerate Disease ActivityOptimize glucocorticoid / immunosuppressive dosing
> 10 pointsHigh Disease ActivityUrgent escalation: pulse methylprednisolone, cyclophosphamide, or mycophenolate
4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures active immune-mediated inflammation in organs such as kidneys, skin, joints, and central nervous system.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Bombardier C, Gladman DD, Urowitz MB, et al. (Arthritis Rheum. 1992;35(6):630-640).
