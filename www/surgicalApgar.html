Surgical Apgar Score (SAS)

Calculation ID: surgicalApgar
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of EBL score + Lowest MAP score + Lowest Heart Rate score (0 to 10 points)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • EBL: Estimated blood loss.
  • Lowest MAP: Mean arterial pressure during surgery.
  • Lowest HR: Intraoperative heart rate.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Surgical Apgar Score (SAS) is a simple 10-point intraoperative scoring system derived from estimated blood loss, lowest mean arterial pressure, and lowest heart rate. It provides an immediate postoperative assessment predicting 30-day major surgical complications and mortality.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Immediate Postoperative Risk Stratification

Key Medical Decision: Identifies high-risk surgical patients (score ≤4) who benefit from postoperative ICU or HDU monitoring.

Reflects intraoperative hemodynamic stability, surgical blood loss, and physiological stress.
