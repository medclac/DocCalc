CDC Surgical Wound Classification

Calculation ID: surgicalWoundClass
1. Formula & Parameters
Categorizes operative wounds into Classes I through IV based on microbial contamination level
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Class I: Clean. Class II: Clean-Contaminated. Class III: Contaminated. Class IV: Dirty-Infected.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The CDC surgical wound classification system categorizes operative incisions based on intrinsic microbial contamination at the time of surgery. This classification predicts surgical site infection (SSI) risk and dictates appropriate antibiotic prophylaxis and wound closure strategies.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Surgical Site Infection (SSI) Prevention
  • Operative Wound Management

Key Medical Decision: Guides antibiotic prophylaxis administration and decisions regarding primary versus delayed primary wound closure.

Reflects bacterial load and tissue contamination within the surgical field.
