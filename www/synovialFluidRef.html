Synovial Fluid Cell Count Interpretation

Calculation ID: synovialFluidRef
1. Formula & Parameters
Differential categorization based on Synovial WBC count and Polymorphonuclear (PMN) percentage
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Normal: <200 WBC. Non-inflammatory: 200–2,000. Inflammatory: 2,000–50,000. Septic: >50,000 cells/µL (>75% PMN).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Synovial fluid analysis via arthrocentesis is the definitive diagnostic test for evaluating joint effusions. Categorizing synovial white blood cell counts and differential percentages separates non-inflammatory arthrosis from inflammatory crystal arthropathies and destructive bacterial septic arthritis.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Monoarticular and Polyarticular Joint Effusions
  • Septic Arthritis vs Gout Triage

Key Medical Decision: Differentiates septic arthritis requiring urgent surgical washout from crystal and degenerative arthropathies.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Quantifies leukocytic infiltration into the synovial cavity in response to infection, microcrystals, or immune-mediated synovitis.
