Estimated Total Daily Insulin (TDD) Helper

Calculation ID: tddInsulinHelper
1. Formula & Parameters
Weight (kg) × Factor (0.3 – 1.0 units/kg/day)
Normal Range / Target: 0.5 units/kg/day

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Weight: kg.
  • Phenotype Factor: 0.3 to 1.0.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Estimates initial total daily insulin requirements based on body weight and clinical phenotype [cite: ADA Standards of Care, 2024].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Insulin Initiation [cite: ADA Standards of Care, 2024]

Key Medical Decision: Calculates starting daily insulin dose [cite: ADA Standards of Care, 2024].
