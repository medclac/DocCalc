Thoracolumbar Injury Classification and Severity Score (TLICS)

Calculation ID: tlicsSpine
1. Formula & Parameters
Sum of Injury Morphology + Posterior Ligamentous Complex (PLC) Integrity + Neurological Status
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Morphology: Compression (1), Burst (2), Translation (3), Distraction (4). PLC: Intact (0) to Disrupted (3). Neurology: Intact (0) to Incomplete/Cauda Equina (3).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Thoracolumbar Injury Classification and Severity Score (TLICS) provides a standardized, mechanism-based scoring system for thoracolumbar spinal trauma. By evaluating injury morphology, posterior ligamentous complex integrity, and neurological status, TLICS guides evidence-based decisions between non-operative bracing and surgical stabilization.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Thoracolumbar Spinal Trauma Management

4. Pathophysiological Background

Quantifies mechanical stability of the three spinal columns and risk of progressive deformity or neurological deficit.
