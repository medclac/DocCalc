Colorectal Cancer TNM Staging (AJCC 8th Edition)

Calculation ID: tnmColorectal
1. Formula & Parameters
Pathological anatomical staging based on Tumor depth (T), Lymph nodes (N), and Metastasis (M)
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • T: Depth of invasion (Tis to T4b). N: Regional nodes (N0 to N2b). M: Distant metastasis (M0 to M1c).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The AJCC 8th edition TNM staging system for colorectal cancer provides definitive anatomical categorization guiding prognosis and adjuvant chemotherapy (FOLFOX/CAPOX) recommendations for Stage III and high-risk Stage II colon and rectal cancers.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Quantifies local bowel wall penetration, lymphatic dissemination, and hematogenous metastasis.
