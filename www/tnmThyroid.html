Differentiated Thyroid Carcinoma TNM (AJCC 8th)

Calculation ID: tnmThyroid
1. Formula & Parameters
Age-stratified staging (cut-off 55 years) for papillary and follicular thyroid cancer
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Age Cutoff: 55 years. T: Primary tumor size/extension. N: Cervical nodes. M: Distant metastasis.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The AJCC 8th edition thyroid cancer staging system introduces a vital age cutoff of 55 years, significantly shifting downstage classifications for younger patients to prevent overtreatment while preserving accurate prognostic stratification for older adults.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Thyroid Carcinoma Staging & Surgical Management

Key Medical Decision: Guides extent of thyroidectomy (lobectomy vs total thyroidectomy) and radioactive iodine administration.

Age GroupStageTNM DefinitionPrognosis & Surgical Strategy
< 55 YearsStage IAny T, Any N, M0Excellent >98% 10-year survival; total thyroidectomy or lobectomy ± RAI
< 55 YearsStage IIAny T, Any N, M1Distant metastasis present; total thyroidectomy + radioactive iodine (RAI)
≥ 55 YearsStage IT1–T2 N0 M0Low risk; thyroid lobectomy or total thyroidectomy
≥ 55 YearsStage IIT1–T2 N1 M0 OR T3 Any N M0Intermediate risk; total thyroidectomy + neck dissection + RAI
≥ 55 YearsStage III / IVT4a–T4b OR M1High risk; radical surgical resection + adjuvant RAI / external beam RT
4. Pathophysiological Background

Measures local tumor invasiveness into strap muscles/recurrent laryngeal nerve, cervical lymphatics, and distant spread.
