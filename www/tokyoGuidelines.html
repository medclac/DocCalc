Tokyo Guidelines 2018 (TG18) Acute Cholecystitis

Calculation ID: tokyoGuidelines
1. Formula & Parameters
Diagnostic criteria (A+B+C) and 3-tier severity stratification (Grade I, II, III)
Normal Range / Target: Grade I (Mild Acute Cholecystitis)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Diagnostic criteria: Local signs (A) + Systemic signs (B) + Imaging findings (C). Severity based on organ dysfunction and local inflammation.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Tokyo Guidelines (TG18) provide standardized diagnostic criteria and severity grading for acute cholecystitis. Grading severity into mild, moderate, or severe guides timely decisions regarding urgent laparoscopic cholecystectomy versus percutaneous gallbladder drainage in high-risk patients.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Cholecystitis Triage & Management

Key Medical Decision: Determines optimal timing of laparoscopic cholecystectomy versus urgent percutaneous cholecystostomy drainage.

Grade III (Severe)Severe complicated cholecystitisOrgan dysfunction presentICU resuscitation, urgent Percutaneous Transhepatic Gallbladder Drainage (PTGBD), delayed cholecystectomy
4. Pathophysiological Background

Bile duct obstruction leading to increased intra-gallbladder pressure, mucosal ischemia, chemical inflammation, and secondary bacterial infection.
