MDS-UPDRS Part III Motor Score

Calculation ID: updrsMotor
1. Formula & Parameters
Standardized clinician-rated motor examination score in Parkinson's disease (0 to 132)
Normal Range / Target: 0 Points

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Quantifies objective motor signs including rigidity, tremor, bradykinesia, and gait impairment.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Movement Disorder Society-sponsored Revision of the Unified Parkinson's Disease Rating Scale (MDS-UPDRS) Part III is the gold standard clinical rating instrument for quantifying objective motor signs in Parkinson's disease. It provides a granular evaluation of motor subscores across limbs and axial structures.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Parkinson's Disease Clinical Management & Research

Key Medical Decision: Measures motor fluctuation severity, response to dopaminergic medications, and deep brain stimulation efficacy.

Evaluates basal ganglia motor circuit dysfunction and nigrostriatal degeneration.
