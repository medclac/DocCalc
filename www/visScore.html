Vasoactive-Inotropic Score (VIS)

Calculation ID: visScore
1. Formula & Parameters
VIS = Dopamine + Dobutamine + (100×Epi) + (10×Milrinone) + (10,000×Vaso) + (100×Norepi)
Normal Range / Target: VIS = 0 (No vasoactive support)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Dopamine & Dobutamine: 1 mcg/kg/min = 1 pt each.
  • Epinephrine & Norepinephrine: 1 mcg/kg/min = 100 pts each.
  • Milrinone: 1 mcg/kg/min = 10 pts.
  • Vasopressin: 1 unit/kg/min = 10,000 pts.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Quantifies the magnitude of cardiovascular pharmacological support; high VIS scores strongly correlate with mortality and organ failure in septic and cardiogenic shock.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Septic Shock
  • Cardiogenic Shock
  • Post-Cardiopulmonary Bypass Low Cardiac Output Syndrome

Key Medical Decision: Guides escalation of cardiovascular support, addition of stress-dose steroids, or evaluation for mechanical circulatory support (VA-ECMO).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects myocardial depression and systemic vasodilation mediated by inflammatory cytokines, nitric oxide synthase activation, and autonomic dysfunction.

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Validated by Davidson, Tong, Hancock et al. (Pediatr Crit Care Med, 2012) as a robust predictor of morbidity and mortality in critically ill infants and children.
