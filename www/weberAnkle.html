Danis–Weber Ankle Fracture Classification

Calculation ID: weberAnkle
1. Formula & Parameters
Anatomical classification (Type A, B, C) relative to distal tibiofibular syndesmosis
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Type A: Infrasydesmotic. Type B: Trans-syndesmotic. Type C: Suprasydesmotic.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Danis-Weber classification categorizes fibular malleolar fractures relative to the distal tibiofibular syndesmosis. Identifying whether a fracture is infrasyndesmotic (Weber A, stable), trans-syndesmotic (Weber B, variable stability), or suprasyndesmotic (Weber C, unstable with syndesmotic rupture) guides surgical fixation versus casting.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Ankle Fractures & Syndesmotic Injury

Key Medical Decision: Determines ankle fracture stability and indications for surgical stabilization (ORIF) and syndesmotic screw/suture-button fixation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Rotational forces (supination-external rotation, pronation-abduction) transmitting energy across the ankle mortise.
