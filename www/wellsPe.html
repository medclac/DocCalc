Wells Score for Pulmonary Embolism

Calculation ID: wellsPe
1. Formula & Parameters
Additive scoring of clinical criteria for suspected pulmonary embolism
Normal Range / Target: ≤ 4.0 points (PE Unlikely)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Scores DVT signs (+3), alternative diagnosis less likely (+3), tachycardia (+1.5), immobilization/surgery (+1.5), prior VTE (+1.5), hemoptysis (+1), malignancy (+1).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The Wells score is the most widely utilized clinical pretest probability model for pulmonary embolism (PE). It stratifies patients into 'PE Unlikely' versus 'PE Likely' categories, directing the efficient and safe use of high-sensitivity D-dimer testing versus computed tomography pulmonary angiography (CTPA).

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Suspected Acute Pulmonary Embolism
  • Venous Thromboembolism Diagnostic Workup

Key Medical Decision: Directs diagnostic strategy between high-sensitivity D-dimer testing and urgent CT pulmonary angiography (CTPA).

4. Pathophysiological Background

Quantifies clinical risk factors for venous stasis, endothelial injury, and hypercoagulability leading to pulmonary vascular occlusion.
