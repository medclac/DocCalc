Westley Croup Score

Calculation ID: westleyCroup
1. Formula & Parameters
Total Score = Stridor (0-2) + Retractions (0-3) + Air Entry (0-2) + Cyanosis (0-5) + LOC (0-5)
Normal Range / Target: Score 0 = Normal Airway

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Inspiratory Stridor: None (0), with agitation (1), at rest (2).
  • Chest Retractions: None (0), mild (1), moderate (2), severe (3).
  • Air Entry: Normal (0), decreased (1), severely decreased (2).
  • Cyanosis: None (0), with agitation (4), at rest (5).
  • Level of Consciousness: Normal (0), lethargic/disoriented (5).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Quantifies upper airway obstruction severity in acute laryngotracheobronchitis, guiding appropriate corticosteroid and nebulized L-epinephrine therapy.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Acute Laryngotracheobronchitis (Croup)
  • Subglottic Edema
  • Viral Upper Respiratory Infection

Key Medical Decision: Guides outpatient Dexamethasone prescription, ED observation, or emergent PICU admission and difficult airway preparation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Viral infection (parainfluenza, influenza) causes inflammation, edema, and mucous exudate in the subglottic region of the trachea. Because the pediatric cricoid ring is the narrowest part of the airway, even modest mucosal edema exponentially increases airway resistance (Poiseuille's Law).

5. Medical Background & Evidence

Originally published by Westley, Cotton, and Brooks (Am J Dis Child, 1978) to standardize croup severity assessment.
