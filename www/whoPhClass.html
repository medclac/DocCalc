WHO Functional Assessment of Pulmonary Hypertension

Calculation ID: whoPhClass
1. Formula & Parameters
4-tier functional limitation classification (WHO Class I to IV) adapted from NYHA
Normal Range / Target: Normal Range

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Grades physical limitation and symptom severity from asymptomatic (I) to right-heart failure at rest (IV).
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

The WHO functional classification of pulmonary hypertension is the primary clinical tool for grading functional impairment in pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH). Functional class directly dictates therapeutic escalation—from oral monotherapy/combination therapy in Class II/III to continuous parenteral prostacyclins in Class IV.

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension (PAH) Staging

Key Medical Decision: Guides targeted pulmonary vasodilator therapy selection and lung transplant evaluation.

4. Pathophysiological Background

Reflects right ventricular adaptation and functional capacity in the face of elevated pulmonary vascular resistance.
