Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR)

Calculation ID: whrCalc
1. Formula & Parameters
Waist (cm) ÷ Hip (cm)
Normal Range / Target: <0.90 (M) / <0.85 (F)

Formula Breakdown & Units:

  • Waist & Hip: cm.
2. Clinical & Medical Significance

Differentiates visceral from subcutaneous fat [cite: WHO Report, 2008].

3. Useful Conditions & Medical Decisions

Useful Conditions / Indications:

  • Central Adiposity [cite: WHO Report, 2008]

Key Medical Decision: Assesses cardiometabolic risk [cite: WHO Report, 2008].

SexAt-Risk Threshold
Male>0.90 [cite: WHO Report, 2008]
Female>0.85 [cite: WHO Report, 2008]
4. Pathophysiological Background

Visceral adipose tissue accumulation [cite: WHO Report, 2008].

5. Medical Background & Evidence

World Health Organization (Waist Circumference and Waist-Hip Ratio: Report of a WHO Expert Consultation. 2008) [cite: WHO Report, 2008].
